# 3-Month YouTube Roadmap — Incremental Games Channel
 
Oct 6, 2026 · @Mamunur Rashid
 
Day 1 is Wednesday 7 October 2026 and the plan runs 13 weeks, to Tuesday 5 January 2027. It builds on the niche research: long-form incremental, idle and satisfying-sim Steam games with your voice, recorded on your Mac mini M4.
 
## At a glance

The check targets are goals I set to match the mid case in the niche research, not guarantees. The 5 January check matters most because the watch-hour bar doubles for new applicants on 1 February 2027.
 
## Days 1 to 7: Mac mini and channel setup
 
By Sunday 11 October your Mac mini records clean video plus your voice, the channel is live, and the first video is published. Your Mac mini M4 (16 GB) is enough for these games; you don't need a new PC. The Windows PC without a graphics card can stay a backup editing machine.
 
### What to get
 
| Item | Why | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| USB microphone (any well-reviewed budget USB condenser or dynamic mic) | The Mac mini has no microphone, and your voice is what makes the channel monetizable | One-time |
| Headphones, wired | Stops game sound leaking into the mic | Use what you have |
| External SSD, 1 TB (if your Mac has the base storage) | An hour of 1080p60 recording is several GB; keep raw footage off the system drive | One-time |
| [CrossOver](https://www.codeweavers.com/crossover) | Runs Windows-only Steam games | Free 14-day trial, then about $74 a year |
| Steam games | See the game list below | $3 to $20 each; Next Fest demos are free |
 
Your mouse is enough; these games don't need the Fantech controller.
 
### Software (all free)
 
- **OBS Studio** for recording. Use the macOS Screen Capture source for the game window, add your mic as a separate audio track, record at 1080p and 60 fps with the Apple hardware encoder.
- **DaVinci Resolve** (free version, runs natively on Apple Silicon) or **CapCut desktop** for editing. Mainly you will cut dead time and add a few captions.
- **Canva** for thumbnails and channel art.
- **Steam for Mac**, plus Windows Steam inside CrossOver for Windows-only games.
- **vidIQ** browser extension to check keywords and competitors.
### Day by day
 
Weekday tasks fit in one evening hour; the recording and editing sit on your Saturday and Sunday.
 
**Day 1, Wed 7 Oct: create the channel (1 hour)**
 
- [ ] Create the channel with a Brand Account and pick a name and handle that say "idle" or "incremental", without any game or company name in it
- [ ] Verify your phone number in YouTube Studio; that unlocks uploads over 15 minutes and custom thumbnails
- [ ] Upload defaults: category Gaming, language English, audience "No, it's not made for kids"
- [ ] Order the microphone (and SSD if needed)
**Day 2, Thu 8 Oct: OBS (1 hour)**
 
- [ ] Install OBS, give it Screen Recording and Microphone permission in macOS Settings
- [ ] Buy Cookie Clicker (native on Mac) and record a 5-minute test; check that game sound and your voice are both clear and in sync
**Day 3, Fri 9 Oct: CrossOver (1 hour)**
 
- [ ] Start the CrossOver trial and install Windows Steam inside it
- [ ] Test A Game About Digging a Hole and Keep on Mining!; note any game that won't run and drop it
**Day 4, Sat 10 Oct: first recordings (5 to 6 hours)**
 
- [ ] Record video 1: Cookie Clicker, about 60 minutes of play with commentary
- [ ] Record video 2: A Game About Digging a Hole
- [ ] Edit video 1 down to 25 to 40 minutes; make its thumbnail
**Day 5, Sun 11 Oct: publish (4 to 5 hours)**
 
- [ ] Edit video 2 and make its thumbnail
- [ ] Channel banner, profile picture, and a description with a business email for developers
- [ ] Publish video 1 at 12 PM US Eastern (10 PM Dhaka); schedule video 2 for Tuesday at the same time
**Day 6, Mon 12 Oct: study (1 hour)**
 
- [ ] Watch one video each from Azrilich, CG Grows and Nerfed Reflexes (links below); write down how each title, thumbnail and first 30 seconds work
**Day 7, Tue 13 Oct: first check**
 
- [ ] Video 2 goes live; reply to every comment on both videos
- [ ] Time yourself: if one video took more than 5 hours, simplify the edit (fewer cuts, no fancy effects)
When you later join the Partner Program, AdSense will ask for US tax information. Revenue from US viewers is subject to US withholding; check Google's tax help page for the rate that applies to Bangladesh before you file. I did not verify the rate.

## Month 3: scale for the holidays (2 Dec to 5 Jan)
 
Month 3 aims for **15 to 18 long videos**: 3 a week, plus a fourth on Sunday whenever you have a spare recording. The holidays are when US viewers have the most free time, and the year-end lists reuse footage you already have.
 
| Week | Dates | Videos | What to make | Focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 9 | 2 to 8 Dec | 3 to 4 | 2 newest games from scouting; Supermarket Simulator episode 4 or Rock Crusher 100% | Keep the winning format |
| 10 | 9 to 15 Dec | 3 to 4 | 2 new games; one 90-minute relaxing video (Cast n Chill, or whichever relaxing game did best) | Watch hours |
| 11 | 16 to 22 Dec | 3 to 4 | 2 new games; "The 10 best incremental games of 2026" made from your own footage | Year-end search traffic |
| 12 | 23 to 29 Dec | 3 to 4 | Holiday-themed or winter incremental games; a 2-hour "relaxing incremental games to fall asleep to" made from your own footage plus new commentary | US holiday viewing |
| 13 | 30 Dec to 5 Jan | 3 | "Incremental games to watch in 2027" (upcoming Steam pages and demos); 2 new games | The 5 January decision |
 
Compilations must carry new voice-over or commentary. Re-uploading old footage unchanged risks the reused-content rules.
 
**Month 3 to-do:**
 
- [ ] Start one live stream a week if your videos average 1,000 or more views: Saturday 8 to 10 PM US Eastern is Sunday 7 to 9 AM in Dhaka. Public replays count toward watch hours.

- [ ] **Decision, 5 Jan:** at 500+ subscribers and 3,000+ watch hours, put January into hitting 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours so you can apply before 1 Feb 2027. Otherwise keep the pace and aim for the 8,000-hour bar.

## Weekly routine and Sunday checks
 
The routine takes about 14 to 16 hours a week, most of it on your Saturday and Sunday. That is my estimate; time yourself in week 1 and adjust.
 
| Day | Time | Task |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | 30 min | Reply to comments; note any game viewers ask for |
| Tuesday | 45 min | Edit the third video of the week; video goes live at 10 or 11 PM |
| Wednesday | 45 min | Finish the third edit, make its thumbnail |
| Thursday | 30 min | Scouting: pick next weekend's games (see the game list); video goes live |
| Friday | 30 min | Download and test next week's games, in CrossOver if needed |
| Saturday | 6 h | Record 3 games (about 1 hour each with commentary); edit one; video goes live |
| Sunday | 5 h | Edit the second, make thumbnails, schedule; run the checks below |
 
**Every Sunday in YouTube Studio:**
 
- [ ] Click-through rate: 4% or more for a new channel; if lower, redo the thumbnail and title on the weakest video
- [ ] Average view duration: aim for 25% or more of each video; if lower, tighten the first minute
- [ ] Subscribers gained per video: make more of the type that wins
- [ ] Top countries: US, UK, Canada and Australia should be growing as a share of viewers
- [ ] Running totals for subscribers and watch hours against the next check
- [ ] Traffic sources: "Suggested videos" growing means your videos are being shown next to competitors', which is good
## After month 3: January to March 2027
 
What you do next depends on the 5 January decision.
 
| If on 5 Jan you have... | Then in January | Then February to March |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 500+ subscribers and 3,000+ watch hours | Go to 4 videos a week plus a weekly live, all on your best format, to reach 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours by 31 Jan; apply the day you qualify | Set up AdSense and US tax info; turn on mid-rolls for every video over 8 minutes; pitch developers for paid features |
| Less than that | Keep 3 videos a week; switch more slots to 60 to 90 minute videos, since each viewer adds more watch time | Aim for 1,000 subscribers and the new 8,000-hour bar; at 500 subscribers and 3,000 hours, apply for the fan-funding tier (memberships, Super Thanks) |
 
**Other moves for months 4 to 6:**
 
- **Spin-off test:** one Roblox incremental or "+1 every second" video a week, the format Melon and Sunny Games grew to 81K subscribers with. It reuses your number-go-up style and runs natively on your Mac; keep it aimed at teens, not young kids.
- **Mac angle:** an occasional "Can the M4 Mac mini run these Steam games?" video. "Mac gaming" searches are 36% from the US, and it sets you apart from Windows-based competitors.
- **Developer income:** once you average 5,000+ views a video, quote developers a fee for coverage of their launch. I could not verify typical rates; ask other small creators in the niche.
- **Gear upgrade** only once income starts: a better microphone first, then more storage.
