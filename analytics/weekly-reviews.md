# Weekly reviews

Every Sunday (~20 min). Run `/weekly-review` — Claude will ask for the numbers and fill this in.

Where to find numbers in YouTube Studio:
- **Channel:** Analytics → Overview (views, watch time, subs) — set "Last 7 days".
- **Per video:** Content → video → Analytics → Reach (impressions, CTR, traffic sources) and Engagement (average view duration, retention at 30 s and 5 min).
- **YPP progress:** Earn tab.
