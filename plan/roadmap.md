# Roadmap: Oct 2026 – Jan 2027

Last updated: 13 Oct 2026. Week-by-week details: [calendar.md](calendar.md).

## Goal for these 3 months

Not money yet. The goal is to **find what works** and build a machine you can keep running:
- 30+ long videos published
- 1–2 "hit" videos (3x or more your normal views)
- A title style, game type and length that clearly win
- A routine that does not burn you out

The targets below are **guesses** for a brand-new channel. Replace them with your real averages after 10–20 videos.

## YPP path (honest)

- From 1 Feb 2027 new applicants need **1,000 subs + 8,000 watch hours** (365 days). This is Brimlo's bar.
- 8,000 h ≈ **one video with ~36,000 views** at 90 min, or ~340 views per video across 104 videos.
- Without a hit: likely 12–18 months. With one hit: 3–6 months.
- After YPP: AdSense (wire transfer to Bangladesh) + **W-8BEN** form claiming the US–Bangladesh treaty (10% on US earnings).
