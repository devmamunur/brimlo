## Game in one sentence

> The Sun went dark, so every night I launch fireworks, pop lanterns and set off chain reactions — every upgrade is a new star in the sky.

- Video policy checked: no video rules on the Steam page.
- Theme: cozy incremental, 2D pixel art, night sky, fireworks.
- ⚠️ **Flashing lights:** the store page has a photosensitivity warning. In Settings, turn on **"Reduce flashing"** (if it still looks good in the test). It is safer for viewers.
- ⚠️ **Grand Finale:** you can put a night's embers on one giant shell ("Dud or Dragon?"). Fine to try once, but don't say "bet" or "gamble" — say "the big finale shell". It may not be in the demo.
- ⚠️ **The dark Sun** is the end of the full game. It may **not** be in the demo. Don't promise "I fixed the Sun" in the title or the ending unless you see it.
- AI art + AI music (store disclosure). Fine for us; don't comment on it.

### Running joke: "Wake up, Sun."

The Sun is dark, and I keep trying to wake it up with my booms. Say it only after a really big boom.

- First big boom: "Wake up, Sun! …No? Okay."
- A big chain reaction: "Sun, are you watching this? Still sleeping."
- A shell does nothing: "Even the Sun is laughing at that one. Oh wait, it's sleeping."
- A huge sky full of fire: "If this doesn't wake you up, Sun, nothing will."
- Near the end: "Okay, Sun. I'll come back for you in the full game."

Use it 4–5 times, not more. Don't reuse: "Child's play" (The Pool Problem), "My rats are my best workers" (Night of the Idle Horde), "Too big to carry" (Big Ice).
