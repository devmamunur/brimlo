## Game in one sentence

> I'm behind one small wooden wall with one pistol. Zombies come in waves. Every zombie I shoot gives scrap, and scrap buys upgrades, until my pistol shoots lightning and I beat the giant gate boss.

- Video policy checked: not yet (many channels already post videos; the developer links them on Steam)
- Theme: dark/horror · swarm/army (zombie horde)

### Research block

Game facts (from 2 transcripts, read Wed 11 Nov 2026: Ash Swoon 1:01:50, 34.6K views [ziiEMZ9t0j4], Yippee Ki Yay Mr Falcon 16:59 (edited), 108K views [IDKjUd_Ia8A] — check on your screen):
- **Start:** you get **2,000 scrap**. Click the **truck** to open the upgrade menu. Buy the wall first. Then press start.
- **Shoot:** move the mouse over the zombies and my guy shoots. There is also an **auto aim** switch.
- **One run** = zombies walk to the wall and hit it. When the wall breaks, the run ends. Back to base, spend the scrap, try again.
- **Scrap** = the money. More kills = more scrap. Big zombies give much more (Ash: about 6,000–8,000 each).
- **Upgrades (truck):** more damage, faster shooting, more wall health, crit chance → crit damage, **explosive bullets** (blast), **electric bullets** (lightning that jumps from zombie to zombie, "arc"), wall spikes, wall repair over time, wall armor, more scrap per kill, **more zombie spawn points** (more zombies = more scrap), **second survivor** (a friend joins the wall, about 2,000–2,700 scrap), "execution" (finish zombies under 20% health), "second wind" (a broken wall comes back once).
- **Turret:** one turret spot on the wall in the demo (Ash: 2,700 to build). It shoots on its own. Weak against the big zombies.
- **Workshop:** craft a new gun with scrap (the "Scrapper", about 12,000). Gun upgrades are a random choice: crit chance / fire rate / reroll.
- **The horde bar** at the bottom: it fills while you survive. Gold marks = a new zombie type: normal zombies → zombies with hats (2x health) → fat zombies → giant zombies ("Juggernauts") → **the gate boss** at the end.
- **End of the demo:** kill the gate boss → "Act complete. The gate boss falls." Not in the demo: SMG, more turrets, the third survivor (Mia).
- Small things to see: a rubber duck on the map (does nothing), a "RIP" sign.

### Before you press record

1. Install the free demo (app 5402280) in **CrossOver Steam**. Play it for 10 minutes. Check the mouse shooting works.
2. If you played in the test, **start a new save** (or delete the test save) before OBS. The video must start with 2,000 scrap and no wall.
3. Set graphics, sound and language now. Sound at about 40, music at about 30 (Ash had to change it while recording).
4. Start OBS when the base and the truck are on screen.
5. **Don't read the help text** ("Build your wall. Click the truck…"). Just do it.
6. In the first 2 minutes, buy only the wall. Don't read the upgrade menu aloud. Say upgrades in 2–3 words.
7. Turn **auto aim off** at the start (you aim with the mouse, it's more fun). Turn it on later for long runs.
8. Take screenshots: the small wall at the start, the first lightning, the first giant zombie, the gate boss, the "Act complete" screen.

### Running joke: the zombies are "visitors"

The zombies keep coming to my wall like guests nobody invited. I talk to them like a tired house owner. Say only what happens on screen.

- First zombies at the wall: "Sorry, no visitors today."
- More of them: "More visitors. I didn't invite anybody."
- Hat zombies come: "Oh, they wear hats now. Fancy visitors."
- A giant zombie comes: "Okay… that's a very big visitor."
- Late game, nobody gets close: "See? No visitors. Nice and quiet."

Use it 4–5 times, not more. Don't reuse: "Stay down!" (Wait, They Can Climb?!), the hungry shredder (SHRED A BILLION), the animals and the mushroom (Mycelarium).
