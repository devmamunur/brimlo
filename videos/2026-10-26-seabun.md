## Game in one sentence

> A scientist bunny dives in a tiny submarine, shoots sea monsters, and turns the fish it rescues into mounts.

- Theme: survivor-like + incremental (combat). A good break from the cleaning games.
- Competition (vidIQ, 25 Oct 2026): top English video 3.9K views. Korean 10K, Russian 7K.

**How the demo works** (from another creator's full demo video, 25 Oct 2026; check while you play):
- You steer the sub with the **mouse**. Weapons **fire by themselves** at close enemies.
- **Oxygen is your timer and your health.** Getting hit drains it. The dive ends when it runs out.
- Kills drop **bones/scrap**. Spend them in the **upgrade tree** (damage, oxygen, attack speed).
- **Workbench:** more weapon slots, better weapon rarity, enchantments, charms, active and passive modules.
- Weapons you unlock: **harpoon gun** (start), **buzzsaw** (orbits you), **pea shooter**, **laser pistol** (arcs between enemies), **torpedo**, **poison**.
- **Golden enemies** drop **eggs**. Eggs hatch in the **aquarium**: clownfish, dolphin, baby whale, tuna, squid, sea turtle. **Feed** them to turn them into **mounts**. Fish make **pearls** over time (passive income).
- **Legendary shark** egg: about 2% chance. Hard to get.
- Depth goes 100 m → 200 m → 300 m in the **Sunlight Zone**. **Ancient runes**: stand inside them, more enemies come.
- **The boss** comes at the end. Your oxygen is sealed; only hits drain it. It shoots bullet patterns. Beating it **ends the demo**.
