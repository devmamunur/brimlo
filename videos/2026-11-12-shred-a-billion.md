### Research block

Game facts (from 2 transcripts, read Wed 11 Nov 2026: Idle Gamer 1:07:16, 18K views [zbbWMixS5RE], Vey 45:39 [vZIxJ4Ukn2k] — check on your screen):
- **The vault:** a big room full of cash bundles (a bundle = a small stack of bills). The counter at the top shows how many bundles are left: about **104 million** at the start (Idle Gamer saw 104,563,949). Goal on screen: "Clear every last bundle", with a **% cleaned** number (starts at 0%).
- **Main loop:** hold left click to grab bundles → walk to the shredder → drop them in (E drops the whole stack) → the shredder pays money. First drop paid about **$6**.
- **Tools:** at first only **hands** (5 bundles at a time). The **bucket** costs **$1.76 million** — it's the big moment. With the bucket you hold ~200,000 bundles at once, like a vacuum. Shovel, vacuum and mining drones are **full game only**.
- **Two kinds of upgrades:** money upgrades (Tab: grabs in hand, grab speed, bundle value, bag capacity, scoop speed, shredder payout) and **points** (skill tree, you get points when you level up; some skills cost 2–3 points; you can't undo them).
- **Bonus balls** fly around the money (walk into them): **2x money**, **2x speed**, **5x bundle value** (golden touch), **money rain** (money falls from the vents into the shredder), **bottomless bag** (no limit for ~1 minute), **3x shredder**. Golden pallet / gold bars = full game only (useless in the demo).
- **The shredder gets slow** later: money waits in a line to be counted. Shred speed upgrades fix it.
- **Jetpack** later (hold jump to fly). Sprinting is very fast and you can "drift".
- **The last few %** = small bits of money on the floor. Slow and boring (~10–15 min for Idle Gamer). Plan: mow it like a lawn (straight lines). **Speed this up in the edit.**
- Near the end: "New area unlocked: Terrace" (a high floor you can climb). At **100%**: "The vault goes much deeper" (full game teaser) + stats screen ($ earned, bundles shredded, time).
- **Performance:** Vey's game crashed twice and lagged. Set graphics to medium, don't spin the camera very fast. The game saves about every minute.
