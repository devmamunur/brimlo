## Recording script

### Cold open (0:00 – 0:15) — no words

Show 10–15 seconds of the best moment: the sandblaster strips the rust and the item shines. Let the sound play. Say nothing.

### Intro (0:15 – 0:50) — pick ONE, say it slow

**Option 1 — The feeling (calm)**
> "Some games make you stress. This one does the opposite.
> You take something old and ugly, and you make it beautiful.
> It's called Rust and Shine, and the demo is free right now.
> Let's see if it's as relaxing as it looks."

**Option 2 — The question**
> "What is hiding under all this rust?
> Maybe a cheap trinket. Maybe a treasure.
> That's the whole game. You clean, you find out, you sell.
> This is Rust and Shine. Let's find out."

**Option 3 — Short and honest**
> "Rust and Shine isn't out yet, but the demo is free.
> I played it, and I can't stop cleaning things.
> Let me show you why."

### Start of play (0:50 – 5:00)

> "Okay, let's start."
> "So in this game, old rusty things arrive in crates. You clean them, you find out what they are, and you sell them."
> "The goal is simple: earn money, buy better tools, and grow the workshop."
> "Right now I have only a wire brush. Nothing else."
> "Let me open the first crate."
> "I can't see what it is yet. Everything is hidden under the rust."
> *(start cleaning — stay quiet for 5–10 seconds, let the sound work)*
> "I like this sound."
> "Almost clean… and… it's a [item name]."
> "Let's sell it. I get [X]. Not much, but it's a start."
> "Next crate. Same thing."
> *(clean 2–3 more items)*
> "Okay, now I can afford my first upgrade. Let's read the options."
> "This one is [read text]. It costs [X]. I think this is the best one right now, because [it cleans faster]."

### Early game (5 – 20 min) — say these as things happen

- **Reading the screen:** "Let me look at the screen first. Here is my money. Here is my crate. Here are the tools."
- **Tool compare:** "The wire brush is slow but free. The water gun is faster. The sandblaster is the best, but it costs a lot."
- **Waiting for money:** "I need [X] more. Let me clean two more items."
- **Each new item:** "Oh, a [name]. Common. / Oh, this one is rare."
- **Choosing a supplier:** "This supplier is cheap, but the crates are plain. That one is expensive, but maybe better items. I'll take the cheap one for now."
- **A bad crate:** "Hmm, nothing good in this one. That's okay."
- **A good crate:** "Oh, nice. That was worth it."
- **First big sale:** "That one sold for [X]. That's more than the last five items together."
- **Planning:** "My plan is: first a faster tool, then a better supplier, then I think I can get a robot."

### Middle game (20 – 60 min)

- **First station / new workshop floor:** "A new station. I don't have to do everything by hand now."
- **Repair-bots:** "This is the big one. A robot that can feed, clean and sell for me."
- **Watching robots work:** "Look at this. I'm doing nothing, and the money is going up." *(let it run 10–15 seconds, then speed up)*
- **Balance of hand vs robot:** "I'll keep the rare items for myself, and let the robot do the simple ones."
- **Before / after numbers:** "At the start I made [X] per minute. Now I make [Y]."
- **Contracts:** "This is a contract. I need [N] items before the time ends. If I fail, I lose it. If I win, I get a bonus."
- **Bounty:** "This bounty wants one specific item. I'll try to find it."
- **High-pay commission:** "This one needs a perfect restore. It's risky. I'll go slow."
- **Contract result:** "We did it! / I missed it. That's okay, I learned something."
- **Slow part:** "This part is slow. Let's speed it up. I'll be back when something happens."
- **Honest comment:** "One thing I notice: [automation feels slow at the start / manual is faster]. It's not bad, it's just something to know."

### Late game (60 min – end) — only if the demo goes this far

- **Moving workshop:** "I've outgrown this room. Time to move to a bigger workshop."
- **Prestige explained:** "Now I can prestige. It means I reset the workshop, but I keep Renown. Renown buys permanent skills."
- **Choosing skills:** "Three styles: slow and cozy, fully automated, or Legendary hunting. I'll pick [X], because [reason]."
- **Heirloom Gallery:** "This is the Heirloom Gallery. Every treasure I restored stays here forever. That's a nice touch."
- **Second run:** "Second run. Look how fast it is now."
- **If the demo ends:** "And that looks like the end of the demo. It says [read the text]."

### Lines for milestones (quick list — use when they happen)

- First clean item revealed: "Everything is hidden until it's clean. That makes every item a small surprise."
- First upgrade (better tool): "This tool is faster. Before, one item took [X] seconds. Now it takes [Y]. That's a big jump."
- Better supplier / rarer crates: "Better suppliers give me rarer crates. More risk, but maybe more money."
- First rare or Legendary item: "Oh, wow. I didn't expect that. This ugly lump is Legendary."
- First repair-bot (automation): "Now a robot cleans for me. I can watch the workshop run itself while the money climbs."
- Contracts / bounties: "This is a contract. I have a deadline, so I need to be quick. Safe job or big job? I'll take [X]."
- New workshop / station: "I'm outgrowing this bench. Time to move to a bigger workshop."
- Prestige (Renown): "Prestige means I reset, but I keep permanent skills. The next run is faster."
- Big number moment: "Before, I made [10] per minute. Now I make [X]. The numbers are getting big."
- Speed-up (waiting): "This part is slow, so let's speed it up." (then cut or 4x in edit)
- Honest opinion (one time, mid video): "One thing I don't like is [Y]. But the cleaning feels really good."

## Recording tips for the calm style

- Speak slow and low. Short pauses are good here. The cleaning sound is part of the video.
- It is OK to be quiet for 5–10 seconds while you clean. Do not stay silent for minutes: when you have nothing to say, speed it up in the edit.
- Press the OBS chapter hotkey at: first item, first upgrade, first robot, first contract, new workshop, prestige.
- Audio: use the voice preset in [voice-presets.md](../playbook/voice-presets.md). Lower the game sound a little so your voice is clear.
- If the sandblaster or cleaning loop is very long, cut it to a short, clean "satisfying" version, but keep your talking parts.
