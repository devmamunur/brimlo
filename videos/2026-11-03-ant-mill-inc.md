## Game in one sentence

> One ant becomes thousands, and a giant worm under the ground keeps coming to take your food or your ants.

- Video policy checked: **no.** The Steam text says nothing about videos. Many channels have posted it. Check the Steam Discussions in the run test.
- Theme: dark · swarm/army from nothing

## How the game plays (from Haikrazon's transcript and the Steam page, 1 Nov 2026; check it in your own run)

- You do not control the ants. They find food, make trails and carry it home.
- You click near the nest to drop seeds. Food buys upgrades inside the ant hill (Brood Chamber, Forager Hall, Granary, Guard Post, Cutter's Den, Hunger Pit).
- The ant hill grows bigger on the surface when you upgrade the nest.
- Ants tear beetles apart, piece by piece, and carry the pieces home.
- **The worm comes:** it asked for 1,500 food the first time, then 25,000. If you cannot pay, it takes ants.
- Wood walls block new land. Cutter ants open them.
- **The most ants at the end is 3,000** (Mamunur, 2 Nov 2026; a Steam screenshot also shows 2.77K / 3.00K). Say "3,000" at the end only if your screen shows it.
- The demo ended after a giant snail boss: "The demo ends here. The hunger does not."
- Some upgrades are a choice between two options. Keep the pick out loud: "I take this one, because…"
- Each ant killed in a fight can leave an ant soul. The worm also accepts ants instead of food.
- A sign says "Don't go north. Nobody comes back from north." That is where the worm is.
- After the snail, you can call old enemies back for free and keep upgrading. This is how to make the video longer.
