### Before you press record

1. Install the **demo** in CrossOver Steam (it is free; you don't need the full game). Play it for 10 minutes. Does it run smoothly? Does the clicking work?
2. While testing, look for the end of the demo: is there only the prologue + The Compound? Write down how far it goes. Check that the demo has the goal **"Infect 100 humans"**. If it doesn't, change "100 humans" in the first lines to the demo's real goal.
3. Start a **new save** before OBS. Set graphics, sound and language now. Music a bit low.
4. Start OBS when the lab and the body on the table are on screen.
5. **Don't read the story text out loud.** Click through it. Just play.
6. Don't open the upgrade list in the first 2 minutes.
7. **Click the humans a lot** at the start. That's the fun part, and it keeps you alive.
8. Take screenshots: the first zombie in the lab, the first bite, the first horde of 10, a Bloater exploding, the goals list, the end (horde + brains).

### Running joke: "My rats are my best workers."

The rats bring brains every second while my zombies fight. I talk to them like my workers. Say it only when the rats really do something on screen.

- First rats: "Rats! You're my first workers. Bring me brains."
- Rats too slow: "Come on, rats. 0.2 brains? You can do better."
- Upgrade the rats: "Okay rats, you get a raise. Now work harder."
- Horde dies but rats keep working: "My whole horde is gone… but the rats are still working. Good rats."
- Late game, big rats number: "[X] brains a second. My rats are the real heroes of this video."

Use it 4–5 times, not more. Don't reuse: "Stay down!", "Too big to carry" (Big Ice), the toilet (Drink the Ocean), the zombie "visitors" (Wait, They Can Climb?!).
