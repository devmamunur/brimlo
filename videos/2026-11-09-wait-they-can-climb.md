## Game in one sentence

> One man sits on a roof. Thousands of zombies come, and they can climb the walls. You place guns, buy upgrades and try to stay alive for 5 days.

- Video policy checked: not yet (no rule on the Steam page; many channels already post videos)

### Research block

Game facts (from 2 transcripts, read Sun 8 Nov 2026: Ash Swoon 1:10:31 [hrGhZhI5FU4], Defend or Fall 48:29 [dLKMKgNJZ0U] — check on your screen):
- **You (me):** one man alone on a roof. You shoot with the mouse (a rifle that can hit 2 zombies in a line).
- **Points:** every zombie you kill gives points. Points buy upgrades (upgrade screen).
- **You lose a lot.** When the zombies get on the roof, the building health goes to 0 and the day ends. You keep your points. Buy upgrades, then play the same day again. This is the main loop.
- **Win a day = a big bonus** (Ash got 1,000 points for day 1).
- **Days in the demo:** day 1 = 500 zombies · day 2 = 1,000 · day 3 = 2,000 · day 4 = 4,000 · day 5 = 8,000. The full game goes to day 10.
- **Guns you place:** machine gun (first one costs 50 points; you place it on the roof edge and point it down), mortar (shoots a big blast at one spot, ~1,000), walls (+5 walls; zombies must climb them). Max 3 machine guns and 3 mortars in the demo.
- **Your own gun upgrades:** auto fire (no clicking), 2–3 bullets at once, faster reload, assault rifle (later), **electric shots** (late, very strong).
- **Powers:** decoy (press 2, zombies run to it), rapid fire, shockwave (late, pushes zombies away).
- **Zombie types:** normal, red fast ones (day 2), big ones with a helmet (day 3), yellow strong ones, purple ones (day 5).
- **New roads open:** day 3 opens a second road, day 5 a third. Your guns must cover more sides.
- **Locked in the demo:** flamethrower, laser, rocket launcher. Don't plan around them.
- Z key = zombie info. You can place guns again before each day ("remove all").
- **End:** clear day 5 → "Demo complete. You survived every day of the demo version."

### Before you press record

1. Install the demo (app 5484870) in CrossOver Steam. Play it for 10 minutes first (run test).
2. Start a **new save**. Do this **before** OBS.
3. Set graphics, sound and language now. If the game is slow with many zombies, set graphics lower.
4. Start OBS on the first screen with me (the man) on the roof, just before "Start the day".
5. You are the man on the roof. Say "I", never a name.
6. **Don't read the help text** on screen. Just do it.
7. Don't read every upgrade's numbers. Say each upgrade in 3–4 words.
8. When you lose and play the same day again, keep talking or speed it up later. No long quiet parts.
9. Take screenshots: me alone on the roof, the first zombie on the wall, the first mortar blast, the new road, day 5 with 8,000 zombies, "Demo complete".

### Running joke: "Stay down!"

The zombies climb and climb, and I keep telling them to stay down. They never listen. Say it only when they really climb.

- First climber: "Hey! Stay down! Who said you can climb?"
- They climb again: "Stay down! This is my roof."
- One gets up and I lose: "I said stay down. Nobody listens to me."
- The guns on the wall get them: "See? I told you. Stay down."
- Late game, they can't get up: "Stay down. Thank you. Very good."

Use it 4–5 times, not more. Don't reuse: the animals (Mycelarium), the cats (Way Too Many Fish), the girlfriend (Ant Mill Inc), the landlord (Pack Ripper).
