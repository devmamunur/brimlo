## Game in one sentence

> You rip trading card packs open by hand, sell the cheap cards, and fill a binder until you find the golden ticket.

- Video policy checked: **no**. The Steam page says nothing about videos. Check Discussions or the game's Discord before recording.
- Theme: other (card collecting, big card-pack wave in Oct 2026).

## How the game works

From the Steam page, Steam screenshots, the captions of two English videos, and the full ksyliki run transcript (Korean channel, machine translated, sent by Mamunur 31 Oct 2026). Not tested by us. Use the name you see on screen.

Translated names in the Korean transcript: Kisser = Sluggo the Smoocher · Joy-Killer = Buzzkill · Dary/Karl Malone = Legs Malone · Pocket Demon = Pocket Devil · Jungle pack = Canopy pack ($165) · Deep pack = probably Abyss (guess) · "won" = dollars in the game.

**The loop**
- **Rip:** drag along the seal. The game shows a score, for example "99%, perfect rip". A clean rip gives a better card.
- **Sell:** drag cards to the basket, then Checkout.
- **Last card:** cards are sorted by price, so the best card is at the back. The last card can glow blue, purple or yellow. A yellow glow is not always a good card.
- **Pack levels:** selling cards gives pack XP. Slow Mo's Starter pack needed 55 XP for level 2. The next pack unlocks at about level 3 of the last one (Steam screenshot: "Unlocks at Canopy Lvl 3").
- **Packs (prices can change):** Starter $7 → Collector's $50 (same set, better odds) → Canopy $165 → Abyss about $420. Higher packs may follow.
- **Boxes:** at pack level 5. One box = 5 packs, 1 guaranteed foil, 1 golden pack.

**The binder**
- Unlocks at Starter pack level 2, at about minute 3–4. Flick a card left to keep it.
- Each set has 8 cards in the Steam screenshots. Each card has 3 slots: base, foil, Eclipse (purple).
- Cards in the binder seem not to be for sale, but you can take a card out and swap it. Slabs (graded cards) do not go in the binder.
- Better binder quality gives more pack XP (Steam screenshot).
- A finished page gives a golden pack (ksyliki, 56:11). Rarities: common, uncommon, epic, legendary.

**Grading**
- Scores out of 10. Costs 35% of the card's value. **The first one is free.**
- The slab comes back after about 6 packs, not after a set time.
- A high score sells for up to 4 times more. It pays only for Mint or better. A 7 can be a loss.
- The game shows the expected value before you pay. Example from the run: a 10 took one card from $25 to $250.

**Rip Depot (the computer on the table)**
- Upgrades seen: wider rip line (higher rip score) · grading · soft sleeves (cards are not damaged) · gloves (hold and sweep to pick up 3 cards) · third grading slot · express grading · a "raise all prices" upgrade · auto-ripper.
- Prices from the run: third grading slot $400, auto-ripper $1,500.
- Bought upgrades arrive by mail after a few packs. Keep ripping.
- The auto-ripper is slow. Two players said ripping by hand was better. It is good for clearing old packs while you do other things.

**Money**
- Going broke is normal. ksyliki went broke at about 7:40. The game gave him a pack, but it seems to be a debt (9:50).
- Biggest cards in that run: Eclipse legendary about $5,000 raw and $10,000+ graded, top slab $6,250. Yours will be different.

**The end of the demo**
- The real ending is a **golden ticket** screen. It came at about 88 min (MuttleyOS) and 97 min (ksyliki), after the last missing card.
- The last card can take a long time. ksyliki had only one legendary left at 91 min and spent 6 more minutes on it.
- Players do not agree if foil and Eclipse slots are needed. MuttleyOS thought yes. ksyliki said "every type" is enough. Watch the screen.
- The ticket has a **private code** for a raffle of 20 game keys on the game's Discord (enter before 15 Nov 2026, draw 30 Nov, heard in captions). ksyliki showed his code on stream by mistake.

**Not in the demo:** Prestige, Series 2 (the game says they are for the full version). Do not plan for them.

## Recording script

### Before you press record

- Do the 10-minute test. **Test turning the binder page** (a Steam Discussions title says "Cannot turn album page on windows").
- Start a **new game**, so the test save is not in the video.
- Finish settings and language before OBS starts.
- Start OBS with the first pack ready to rip.
- Plan to **hide the golden ticket code**. Cut it or blur it in the edit. If you enter the raffle, type your Steam ID off camera.

### Opening

**First 10 seconds (no cold open, gameplay from the first frame):**

| Time | Say | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "So, I spent all my rent money on one pack of cards." | Rip the pack. |
| 0:04 | "To pay my landlord back, I need the golden ticket." | Show the first card. |
| 0:08 | "For that, I need every card in this game." | Keep looking at cards. |
| 0:11 | "First card: [Sluggo]. Worth [$1]. Okay. I'm in trouble." | Sell it. |

Say it calmly, like it is normal. The story is a joke, and viewers will know that. Use the real name and price of your first card.

**First action (by 0:02):** drag along the seal and rip the first pack.

**Test (31 Oct 2026):** this is our first made-up story opening. 11 of 13 Azrilich videos open with one ([notes](../research/notes/azrilich-voice.md)). It does not say how the video ends. Compare retention at 1:00 with our other videos on Sun 8 Nov 2026.

**Cold open (optional, for `/hook`):** the full binder, then the ticket screen with the code hidden. Max 10 seconds.

> "Every card. 4 sets. And at the end, this."

### Start of play (0:30 – 3:00)

Do this loop until the binder unlocks. Don't read the tutorial text aloud.

**Subscribe line (~2:00):**

> "If you like watching a collection grow, subscribe. I finish a new game every few days."

### Tips from other runs

- **Keep money for the next pack.** All three players ran short of money early.
- **Grade only good cards.** Read the expected value first. Many small grades lost money.
- **Rip by hand** for the best cards.
- **Buy boxes** once they unlock, if you can afford them (guess: ksyliki called them profitable).
- **Speed up the last card hunt** in the edit. It is the slowest part of the video.
- **Press the marker hotkey** at each new pack, each set complete and the ticket.
- **Don't read card jokes in the first 2 minutes.** Later, one funny card name is fine.
