## Recording script

Before recording: play 15 minutes off camera. Learn the buttons (fire, R reload, right click ability, 1–3 switch survivor). Find the real names of the currency, gear screen and upgrades. Replace the `[X]` words below.

### Opening (first 30 seconds — say this after showing 5–15 s of late-game footage)

> Three survivors. One dead city. They fight every wave on their own.
> Can my squad survive hundreds of zombie hordes?
> This demo is free right now. Let's find out.

### Start of play (0:30 – 3:00)

> In this game, you loot a dead city and make your survivors stronger.
> Okay, let's start. This is my bunker. I come back here between runs.
> I have one survivor and a knife. I'm weak right now.
> I pick a street and press deploy. The zombies come from the right.
> I can shoot too. I'll try to help my squad.

### Lines for milestones (use when they happen)

- First loot: "I found my first gun. Let's equip it. I think this is better than the knife."
- First scrap: "I don't need this junk. I'll sell it for scrap."
- First upgrade: "Now I upgrade my survivor. It costs [X], and I have [X]. This one gives more damage."
- First new survivor: "I can recruit a second survivor. This is a big step for my squad."
- First death: "Oh no… okay, it's fine. In this game, dying costs nothing. I keep my gear."
- Enhancement bench: "At the bench I make my gun better. Before, it did [X] damage. Now it does [X]."
- Rare gear: "This is a rare drop. Let me read it. It gives [X]."
- Big jump: "Before, I killed one zombie at a time. Now the whole group dies fast."
- Hard area or boss: "This part is very hard. I need better gear. Let me go back and farm."
- Region cleared: "I cleared this region, and a new one opens."
- Speed-up: "This part is slow, so let's speed it up." / "I'll farm this street again for [X] minutes. Let's skip ahead."
- Thinking out loud: "I think I need more damage before the next area." / "My plan is: first better guns, then a new survivor." / "Hmm, I'm not sure about this one. Let's try it."

### Ending (last 1–2 minutes)

> So that's Outbreak Z. The best part is [X]. The weak part is [Y].
> If you like games where you collect loot and watch your team fight, this one is for you.
> The demo is free on Steam. The full game does not have a release date yet. The link is in the description.
> Thanks for watching. If you want more games like this, subscribe. See you in the next one.

(Do not say a price. It is not announced.)
