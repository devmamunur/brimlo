### Before you press record

1. Open https://mana.gs/pawn-it in **Chrome**. Close other tabs.
2. Play 10 minutes to test: does it run smooth, do upgrades and money really grow, how long is one day? Then start a **new game** before OBS.
3. Set sound and screen size now. Full screen if it runs smooth. Music low before OBS, not on camera.
4. Start OBS when the shop or the first dirty item is on screen.
5. **Don't read the help text** or the emails out loud. Just play. Read only a funny line.
6. Don't open the upgrade menu in the first 2 minutes.
7. **Watch the clock:** if one day is very short and the game ends fast, play for the county board (#1) as the goal. Write your rank and money on paper every ~10 minutes.

### Running joke: "My fake detector."

Every item must be checked for fakes. I call my eyes my "fake detector". Say it only when you really check or get tricked.

- First check: "Okay, fake detector on. Glue? No. Spelling? Good. It's real."
- Catch a fake: "Fake detector says… fake! Nice try, my friend."
- You get tricked: "Oh no. My fake detector is broken today."
- A very strange item (bottled fart / shrink ray): "Fake detector can't help me with this one."
- Late game, fast checks: "Fake detector is a pro now. One look. Real."

Use it 4–5 times, not more. Don't reuse: "Too big to carry" (Big Ice), the toilet (Drink the Ocean), "Please be treasure" (One's Trash).
