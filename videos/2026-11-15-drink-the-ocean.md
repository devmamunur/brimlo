## Recording script

Simple words only. Short lines. Say them slowly. Small mistakes are fine.

### The story of this video

I'm lost on a tiny island. My home is far away, across the ocean. (Mamunur's idea, 14 Nov 2026.)
I have no boat. I have one coconut straw.
So I'm going to drink the whole ocean. Then I can walk home.
Under the water, there is treasure. And maybe something else.
At the end, the ocean will be gone. The game does not show a home — so the payoff is "now I can walk home", said by me, not a game event.

### Before you press record

1. Open **mana.gs/drink-the-ocean** in Chrome. Play 10 minutes to test. Check: does it ask you to sign in or download something? Does it run smooth?
2. Start a **new game** (play alone, not with friends). Do this **before** OBS.
3. Set the sound and the mouse speed now. Make Chrome full screen (no tabs, no bookmarks bar). Turn off Mac notifications.
4. Start OBS on the first screen where you stand on the island with the straw.
5. **Don't read the help text** on screen ("Coastal shallows. The treasure is waiting." etc.). Just play.
6. Don't open the full upgrade list in the first 2 minutes. Buy one thing and go back.
7. Take screenshots: the island at the start, the first whirlpool, the shipwreck, each door (40% and 70%), the end screen with the liters number.

### Running joke: "I need the toilet. Again."

I drink the ocean, so I need the toilet all the time. And the toilet is always far away. Say only what happens on screen.

- First time: "Oh no. My belly is full. Where is the toilet?"
- Again soon: "Toilet again? I was just there!"
- The toilet is far: "Why is the toilet so far away? I'm running here."
- Buying the iron bladder: "Bigger belly? Yes, please. Fewer toilet trips."
- Late game: "I drank half the ocean today. Don't ask how many toilet trips."

Use it 4–5 times, not more. Don't reuse: "Please be treasure" (One's Trash), zombie "visitors" (Wipe Them Out!), "my shredder is always hungry" (Shred a Billion).
