## Game in one sentence

> You have one fingernail and a town full of dusty things. Scrape them clean, sell the dust, and buy a better nail.

- Video policy: not written on the itch page. Many creators already posted videos (25 Oct 2026). No problem expected.
- Theme: **cozy cleanup**. (Rust & Shine is also cleaning, so publish a different theme between them. Mon 26 Oct is the slot for that.)
- Competition (vidIQ, 25 Oct 2026): only tiny videos (10 and 68 views) and one from a 1K-sub channel posted 25 Oct (27 views on day 1). No big channel yet.

**What's in the demo** (itch page): scrape with out-and-back strokes (the game rewards it); the nail wears down; dust goes into a jar and is the currency; skill tree (firmer press, tougher nail, bigger jar, Hot Hands, Clean Sweep, business cards for new clients); **3 hidden finds** under every dusty place (the owner gives a tip); **stamps** = clean a place to its mark in one go → a "Shine" (special currency). Route: ceiling fan, attic window, blinds, a laptop, a piano… Uncle Gary tells the story. The demo ends where the other kinds of gunk begin (gum, soot, burnt cheese, grease come in the full game).

## Recording script

### Opening (play from second 0 — no hello, no menu tour)

Start the recording **already on the first job** (the ceiling fan). Scrape while you say:

> "I have one fingernail. And this whole town is covered in dust. Let's scrape all of it clean."
> "The dust I scrape is my money. More dust, better nail. Let's see how far one fingernail can go."

(Cold open in the edit: put 5–10 s of the **most satisfying clean** from late in the video before this line.)

### Start of play (first 3 minutes)

> "This game is Scrapecremental. It's free in the browser right now."
> "No buttons. I scrape with my mouse. Out and back on one spot works best."
> "Look, the dust goes into this jar. That's my money."
> "My nail gets worse when I use it. So the first upgrade I want is a tougher nail."

**Gary's story text:** don't read it all. Say one line: "Uncle Gary gave me his scraping business. And his radio." Then keep scraping.

### Lines for milestones (use when they happen)

- First upgrade: "First upgrade: a tougher nail. Now it lasts [X] strokes instead of [Y]."
- First hidden find: "Wait, there's something under the dust! … It's a [find]. The owner gives me a tip for it."
- First stamp: "Stamped! I cleaned it in one go. That gives me a Shine, and you can't grind those."
- Bigger jar: "Bigger jar. Now I can carry [X] dust before I have to sell."
- Hot Hands / Clean Sweep: "This one makes every stroke clean much more. Look at that, half the window is gone in one swipe!"
- New client: "Business cards! New customers on the board. Now I can clean a [laptop / piano]."
- Big number: "I just passed [X] dust. At the start, one job gave me [Y]."
- Speed-up (edit): "This part is just more scraping. Let's speed it up." (Never more than ~10 s of quiet.)
- A mistake or crash: "Oh no, the game froze. It's a browser demo, so that can happen. Let me reload. It saves by itself."

### Ending (last 1–2 minutes)

> "That's the whole Scrapecremental demo. The best part is the sound and the feeling when the dust comes off. The weak part: [your honest one, e.g. my wrist hurts / it's short]."
> "If you like cozy cleaning games with upgrades, this one is for you. It's free in your browser, and the full game is coming to Steam in December. Links are in the description."
> "Thanks for watching. Subscribe if you want more games like this. See you in the next one."
