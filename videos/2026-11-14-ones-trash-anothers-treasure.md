### Research block

Game facts (from 1 transcript, read Sat 14 Nov 2026: Idle cub 1:52:12, 111K views [1mzqQ4OixFc] — check on your screen):
- **Start:** "You spent your life savings on every box in this yard." **99,380 boxes**. No refunds. Only the **grinder** can open them.
- **Main loop:** pick up boxes with the **trash bag** (left click) → put them in the **grinder** → it opens them → scrap + sometimes an **item** comes out → throw it all into the **pit** (the big hole) → the pit pays money.
- First trip: 8 boxes → **$88**.
- **Terminal** (press E): the shop. Tools, machines, upgrades. There is also a **skill tree**.
- **Items** are rare. They have colors: common, rare (blue), **legendary** (gold). Idle cub: rare wine $210, legendary boots **$2,000** (at 18:34). There is a **collection log** (sets like "Kitchen 3/3").
- **Scrap** is the small junk. Later some scrap comes out as **metal** (blue) and **gold**.
- **Quests** (top left): "Sweep 800 pieces with the broom" → unlocks **automation**. Others: clear 2,500 boxes, clear 5,000 boxes (unlocks more drones), suck up 1,000 boxes.
- **Tools:** trash bag → **broom** (~$600, push scrap into the pit) → **vacuum wand** ($8,000, sucks boxes and items fast).
- **Machines:** **auto grinder** (pulls in the nearest box every 2 s), **conveyor belt** ($400), **fan**, **launcher** ($1,500, throws things in an arc), **picker arm** (takes boxes from a pile), **second grinder** (~$5,000), **robot sweeper** (a little cleaning robot, "like a Roomba"), **courier drone** (brings boxes to the grinder).
- **End of the demo:** the box counter reaches 0 → "You cleared all the boxes. The pit is full and so are your pockets. The yard is clean, but not yet yours. The deed is still for sale at the terminal. Thanks for playing the demo." Idle cub got stuck at 22 boxes for a minute: the last ones were hidden; look around the yard.

### Running joke: "Please be treasure."

The game says most of it is junk. Every time something comes out of the grinder, I hope it's treasure. It almost never is. Say only what happens on screen.

- First item: "Please be treasure… please be treasure… it's cigarettes. Okay."
- Another junk item: "Please be treasure… no. Junk again."
- A rare (blue) item: "Please be treasure… oh, it's blue! That's a little bit treasure."
- First legendary (gold) item: "Please be treasure… YES! Finally! Real treasure!"
- Late game, machines open everything: "I don't even say please anymore. The robots do it for me."

Use it 4–5 times, not more. Don't reuse: the hungry shredder (SHRED A BILLION — **don't talk to the pit like a hungry pet**), "Stay down!" (Wait, They Can Climb?!), the "Doomsday" giant zombies (Wipe Them Out!), the animals (Mycelarium).
