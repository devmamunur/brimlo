## Game in one sentence

> You pull up a net full of flopping fish, and you must throw every fish into the right crate before you can sell them.

- Video policy checked: **no.** The Steam page says nothing about videos. The developer shares player videos and many channels have posted it. Check Steam Discussions in the run test.
- Theme: cozy · sorting / job-sim with upgrades

## How the game plays (Steam page, 3 Nov 2026; check it in your own run)

- Drop the net anywhere in open water. Pull it up. A pile of fish lands on the deck.
- Grab a fish, find its crate, throw it in. Then sell.
- Junk comes up too: keep it out of the crates. **Protected fish** must go back over the rail.
- The demo has **18 fish species** (the full game has 82). Buy a **license** to unlock each new species. Each one gets its own crate.
- Sail to the island. Spend money at the stall: bigger nets, longer reach, more tricks.
- **The locals on the island are cats, and they love fish.** Find out in the run test what the cats really do. The running joke depends on it.
- The demo is the first region of the full game.
