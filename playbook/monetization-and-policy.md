# Monetization and policy

Facts checked Oct 2026 (sources in the [research report](../research/reports/Idle%20gaming%20channel%20playbook.md)). Re-check before acting — rules change.

## YouTube Partner Program (YPP)

| | Until 31 Jan 2027 | From 1 Feb 2027 (new applicants) |
|---|---|---|
| Subscribers | 1,000 | 1,000 (per secondary sources) |
| Long-form | 4,000 public watch hours / 12 months | **8,000 watch hours / 365 days** |
| Shorts route | 10M Shorts views / 90 days | 20M Shorts views / 90 days |

Source: YouTube Official Blog, 10 Aug 2026. Creators already in YPP are not affected.

- **Brimlo's bar is 8,000 hours.** 4,000 before 1 Feb 2027 is not realistic for a new channel.
- The lower 500-subscriber tier (memberships, Super Thanks) is **not available in Bangladesh**.
- Shorts-feed views don't count toward watch hours. Public live-stream replays do.
- After YPP: 6+ months of no uploads can turn monetization off.

## Watch-hour math

| Video length | Watched per view (~15%, modeled) | Views needed for 8,000 h |
|---|---|---|
| 90 min | 13.5 min | ~35,600 |
| 120 min | 18 min | ~26,700 |

One video with ~36K views at 90 min ≈ 8,000 hours by itself. **YPP here is mostly a "one hit" problem.**

## Policy: what keeps a gameplay channel monetizable

- YouTube: game videos can earn when "commentary is strictly tied to the live action being shown". "Videos simply showing a user playing a video game… for extended periods of time may not be accepted."
- "Inauthentic content" (renamed from "repetitious content", Jul 2025): avoid near-identical videos with minimal commentary.
- **Your risk = long silent idle stretches.** Speed up or cut them. Talk during the parts you keep.
- Give every video a different goal/idea. No copy-paste episodes.
- Check each game's video/monetization policy (Steam page, press kit, Discussions).

## Money expectations

- US long-form gaming RPM ≈ **$2–6 per 1,000 views** (median ~$3.50, third-party estimates).
- 10,000 views/month ≈ $35. 100,000 views/month ≈ $350.
- Mid-roll ads on videos 8+ min; YouTube places them automatically.
- Sponsorships below 1,000 subs are unlikely. Key platforms (Keymailer, Lurkit, Woovit) mostly give free keys.

## Bangladesh: payout and tax

- AdSense pays Bangladesh by **wire transfer**.
- YPP income = "Other Copyright Royalties". US tax only on earnings from US viewers.
- **Submit W-8BEN** (individual) in AdSense and claim the US–Bangladesh treaty → royalties capped at **10%** (verify the rate shown in AdSense).
- **No tax form → up to 24% of ALL earnings withheld.**
- Bangladesh side: banks have deducted 7.5% on foreign service income; the FY2026-27 budget proposed removing it for creators and allowing $5,000 without paperwork — **not confirmed as law**. Check with your bank when payments start.
