## Brimlo title system (Mamunur, Fri 6 Nov 2026) — USE THIS

**This replaces every older rule and template in this file** (the old ones are kept below only for history). Built from Azrilich (50 titles) and LuckyLemon (14 titles), checked 5 Nov 2026. Study: [research/reports/2026-11-06-azrilich-title-patterns.md](../research/reports/2026-11-06-azrilich-title-patterns.md).

### What they do (facts)

| | Azrilich (50 titles, median 92K views) | LuckyLemon (14 titles) |
|---|---|---|
| Game name in title | **0 of 50** | 0 of 14 |
| "incremental", "\|", "Demo" | never | never |
| Length | median **39 characters, 7 words**, max 61 | ~45 characters |
| Voice | 30 of 50 start with **"I"** (first person, past tense) | almost all "I …" |
| Numbers | only 7 of 50 | most titles, in digits |
| Case | all lowercase (his signature) | Title Case |
| CAPS words | almost never | never |

The title is a **tiny story or a joke about what happened**, not a description of the game.

### The 7 Brimlo patterns

| # | Pattern | Real examples (views, theirs) | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **I [did] [BIG NUMBER] [thing] [short twist]** | I can't leave until I pick up 1,000,000 coins (200K) · I got paid to destroy 100 quintillion dollars (274K) · I Farmed 1,000,000,000,000,000 Carrots And I Regret Nothing (45K) | A true big number on screen. **Default for simple games** |
| **P2** | **I [did a crazy thing] [funny result]** / **I [did X] Until I [funny result]** | i flipped bottles until i lost my mind · I scratched lottery tickets until I won · I made the grim reaper look unemployed (604K) · I printed so much money I scared the king (408K) · I sacrificed an entire frog civilization to a hole (187K) | Something in the game reacts, breaks or gets absurd. **Default for complex games** |
| **P3** | **They Made Me [huge job]** / **They Gave Me [tiny tool] and Told Me to [huge job]** | they made me sort the entire ocean (123K) · They Gave Me A Toothbrush And Told Me To Clean The Moon (126K, FungalBrainRot) | Job-sim, cleaning, sorting: a boss gives you the work |

Use Studio **Test & Compare** for titles too when it is available.

Rare extra (max 1 in 10 videos): "This Is the Most Satisfying Game of the Year" (225K) — only if you really mean it.

### Brimlo rules (our own look, not a copy)

1. **Game name: decide by game type** (see "Game name: decide by game type" below). Never the game name first. The game name always goes in the first description line + tags.
2. **Never "Demo", "incremental", "|" or "100%"** (unless you really finished the full game).
3. **Title Case**, not all lowercase (lowercase is Azrilich's signature). **No CAPS words**, or max one.
4. **Short:** aim for **30–55 characters, 5–9 words.**
5. **First person past tense** ("I fed…", "I sorted…") in most titles. Use P3–P7 for variety; do not use the same pattern 3 videos in a row.
6. **True to the video:** every number and claim must happen on screen. Write the final title **after** recording (or check it after). A score never beats the truth.
7. **Own words:** never copy a title from another channel. Copy the *shape*.
8. **Funny is good, mean is not.** A small joke or twist ("And I Regret Nothing", "…Before It Ate Me", "…and the Cats Were Not Happy") from this game's real events.
9. **Thumbnail text never repeats the title** (see thumbnails.md).

**Game name: decide by game type (market check Tue 10 Nov 2026, 24 games — replaces the 7 Nov and 10 Nov exceptions).** Data: [research/reports/2026-11-10-game-name-in-title-check.md](../research/reports/2026-11-10-game-name-in-title-check.md).

| Game type | Title shape | Real examples that won on small English channels |
|---|---|---|
| **Survivor / horde / zombie / roguelite / action** (people search it by name: playtest, hot demo, launch) | **Hook first, game name at the end** after " \| " or " - ", or the name inside the sentence | Ash Swoon (3.6K subs) "This is an Auto-Battler About Surviving Zombie Hordes! \| Outbreak Z" 53K · BryBry (1.1K) "…\| I am Plague Playtest" 22.7K · Sharkinbear (2.3K) "The Only Thing Your Rats Remember Is The Taste Of Flesh And Bone - I Am Plague" 18.6K · our I Am Plague / Outbreak Z / Seabun |
| **Cozy / satisfying / sim / clicker** | **Story title, no game name** (P1–P7) | Haikrazon (4.2K) "We Start With 1 Ant, Then Grow The Colony To Thousands To Consume Everything" 212K · JustPatrick-Idle (4.1K) "Growing a Mushroom Network Until It Takes Over EVERYTHING!" 70K · Slow Mo (2K) "I Ripped Packs Until I Got the Legendary Ones" 13K |

- **Never the game name first:** 0 wins in 24 games (best: 3.5K).
- **Hook first, always.** The first ~40 characters must make a stranger curious; the name comes last.
- **Never copy a winner's title:** SB Atomic used Ash Swoon's exact title → 118 views.
- Shape alone does not make a hit: every shape also has flops under 1K. Timing (launch wave) and thumbnail matter more.
- Our own data: cozy videos got about the same small search traffic with or without the name (description + tags already do it); only the survivor-likes got real search traffic.
- No "Demo" in the title. Review this rule at each /weekly-review with search traffic per video.
- **Exception — old video with a proven name search (Wed 11 Nov 2026):** when you swap the title of an older video (past its first ~7 days) that ranks in the top 3 of YouTube search for the game name, or gets a clear share of its views from Search, **keep " - Game Name" at the end**, whatever the game type. After week 1, Home stops pushing a small video and search is what keeps it alive. Example: Scrapecremental (#2 for its name) → "I Scraped a Dinosaur Skull Clean With My Fingernail - Scrapecremental".
- **These rules are not fixed (Mamunur, 11 Nov 2026).** They are our best guess from today's data. Change a rule only after new market research (20+ games, small and big channels) or our own clear data, and write the date and the reason here.

### Title recipe by game type (Tue 10 Nov 2026 — from the 24-game / 10-channel study)

**Every title starts here.** First fill this card (from the video file, Steam page or recording):

```
Game name:        ...
Game type:        (one row from the table below)
Concept (1 line): I [main action] to [goal]
Hot right now?:   launch/playtest ≤ 48 h, or YouTube wave started ≤ 48 h ago? yes/no
Core fact:        biggest TRUE number · tiny tool vs huge job · end result · funniest moment
```

| Game type (how to know it) | Game name in title? | Hook that wins (pattern) | Real winning titles (channel subs → views) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Survivor / horde / zombie / tower defense / roguelite / action** (enemies, waves, guns, bosses) | **Yes, at the end**: " \| Game Name" or " - Game Name" | **1 me vs BIG NUMBER enemies** · "This is a … About …!" · "How long can I survive" (P1, P5) | Ash Swoon 3.6K → 41K "This is an Incremental Game About Holding Off Climbing Zombies! \| Wait, They Can Climb?!" · Erdem 42K → 64K "1 vs 10,000 Zombies - How Many Days Can I Survive? - Wait, They Can Climb?!" · Sharkinbear 2.3K → 18.6K "The Only Thing Your Rats Remember Is The Taste Of Flesh And Bone - I Am Plague" · (no name also works: IdleAlex 629 → 14K "I Tried to Become a Billionaire by Killing Zombies") |

| **Colony / army / swarm growth** | No | **"Start with 1 X → thousands → consume everything"** (P1) | Haikrazon 4.2K → 212K "We Start With 1 Ant, Then Grow The Colony To Thousands To Consume Everything" · Dex → 104K "I Conquered EVERYTHING with an Army of Ants" · Slow Mo 2K → 37K "Building an Unstoppable Army One Fusion at a Time" |

If a game fits two rows, use the row of its **main loop** (what you do 80% of the time). Not sure → the no-name story title is the safe default (most small-channel breakouts).

### How to make a title (step by step)

1. Fill the card above and pick the **game type row**.
2. Write the core fact in one line (biggest true number, tiny tool vs huge job, end result, funniest moment).
3. **A (live title)** = the row's winning hook with our own words and the core fact. Name only if the row says so.
4. **B** = a different pattern (P1–P7) of the same row. **C** = the name version if the row allows a name (survivor/horde or launch day); otherwise a third pattern.
5. Hook check: the **first ~40 characters** must make a stranger curious without knowing the game (number, absurd tool, dark twist).
6. Check: true on screen? 30–55 characters (a " \| Game Name" tail may go up to ~70)? Title Case, max one CAPS word? Not the same pattern as the last 2 videos? Never the game name first? Not a copy of a real title?
7. Score with `brimlo_score_title` (and vidIQ if useful) **only to compare** — the truth and the row win over a score.

### A/B/C title set (every video) + hand swaps

**Always give 3 titles as an A/B/C set** (Mamunur, 6 Nov 2026). **Since 10 Nov 2026 they are swap titles, not a Studio A/B test:**

```
A (main): …
B: …
C: …
```
- A = the best pick; it is the title the video goes live with. B and C are **swap titles** for later.
- **Each one a different pattern** (P1–P7), so a swap really tries a new idea, not 3 copies of one idea.
- All 3 true to the video, all follow the rules above, each under 100 characters (YouTube limit), aim 30–55.
- One line under each: the pattern + why.

**Hand swap (instead of a Studio A/B test):**
1. Go live with A. Do not start a Studio A/B test.
2. After 2–3 days, check views and impressions CTR in Studio. If the video is below the channel median (or CTR is clearly low), change the title to B by hand.
3. Write the swap date and the old/new title in the video file and in the Title test log below. Wait 2–3 more days before judging B. Swap to C only if B is also weak.

**Why no Studio A/B test now (research, 10 Nov 2026):** YouTube's help page says the most common reason for "Inconclusive" is too few impressions; with no clear winner the first title stays anyway, and changing the title stops the test. Guides say ~1,000–5,000 impressions per title are needed (Derral Eves: 10K+). Tintin Smith (How to YouTube) says test only at ~10,000+ views per video and change titles by hand before that. Our videos get ~30–1,000 views. Sources: [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en), [Gyre](https://gyre.pro/blog/youtubes-new-title-ab-testing-tool-everything-creators-need-to-know), [ThumbnailCreator](https://www.thumbnailcreator.com/blog/thumbnail-a-b-testing-why-results-mislead), [Tintin Smith](https://youtubehowto.substack.com/p/is-ab-testing-useful).

**When to use Studio A/B tests again:** on a video that gets ~1,000+ impressions a week (check Studio), older videos first (YouTube's own advice). Make the titles very different and do not touch the title during the test. Review this rule when videos reach ~10,000 views each.

### Old rules

1. Put **"incremental"** in most titles. ("incremental game" searches +71%; "idle games" −54%.)
2. One clear promise: **tiny → huge**, a strange idea, or a feeling.
3. 1–3 words in CAPS. No emoji. Simple English.
4. **Pure incremental** → sell the idea, put the game name in the description.
5. **Hyped launch / Next Fest demo / playtest / roguelite / tower defense** → put the game name in the title ("| Game Name" at the end, or inside the sentence). **Do not write "Demo" in the title** (Mamunur, 26 Oct 2026: it looks cheap, and it gets old when the full game comes out). 6. Never put other creators' names in titles or tags.
7. Don't copy another channel's exact words. Copy the *shape*.
8. Write 3 options for every video. Test with Test & Compare if your Studio has it.

## "Satisfying" decision (13 Oct 2026)

**Evidence (mild, not proof):**
- Idle Cub top-50: 6 titles say "satisfying" → average **409K** views (median 439K) vs 44 other titles → average **344K** (median 282K). Small sample; the list is his *top* videos, so it is biased toward winners.
- Azrilich: "most satisfying cleaning game ever" 162K (above his usual), "shoot block = max satisfaction" 54K (below).
- vidIQ search: "satisfying game" is mostly Roblox, Minecraft and tutorial searches → it is a **browse/feeling word, not a search keyword**.

**Rules:**
1. Use "satisfying" only when the screen is smooth, repeating progress (crush, mine, clean, sort, automate).
2. Skip it for dark/horror, gambling, challenge/cheat, story and calm/relaxing games — use the matching feeling word (DISTURBING, BROKEN, RELAXING…).
3. Put it **after a concrete idea**, never alone.
4. At most **1 video in 3**. Overuse makes titles look the same.
5. Test: after 10 videos, compare "satisfying" titles vs others (CTR and 7-day views) in the log below.
