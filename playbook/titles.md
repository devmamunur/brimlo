# Titles

The Brimlo title system. Cleaned Thu 19 Nov 2026: old templates (A–J, "two shapes", "put incremental in titles", CAPS feeling words) removed — they are in git history before 19 Nov 2026 if ever needed.

Studies behind it (newest decide):
- [2026-11-06 Azrilich title patterns](../research/reports/2026-11-06-azrilich-title-patterns.md) — 50 + 14 titles, the P1–P7 base
- [2026-11-10 Game name in title check](../research/reports/2026-11-10-game-name-in-title-check.md) — 24 games, 10 channels
- [2026-11-19 MrBeast title patterns](../research/reports/2026-11-19-mrbeast-title-patterns.md) — 187 titles + 39 niche outliers

## What wins in our niche (facts)

| | Azrilich (50 titles, median 92K views) | LuckyLemon (14) | Niche outliers, 19 Nov 2026 (39 videos, channels < 300K) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Game name in title | **0 of 50** | 0 of 14 | rare |
| Voice | 30 of 50 start with **"I"** | almost all "I …" | most top ones start with "I" |
| Length | median **39 characters, 7 words** | ~45 characters | similar |
| Numbers | 7 of 50 | most titles, digits | the big-number ones win (1 MILLION Times 4.8×) |

The title is a **tiny story or a joke about what happened**, not a description of the game.

**Big-entertainment shapes do not win here (19 Nov 2026).** MrBeast titles ("$1 vs $X", "Win $X", "$X Every…", "100 People Fight…") are 0 of 39 niche outliers. The niche winners are first-person story titles: "I Didn't Know Numbers Went This High..." (25×), "I Hired an Army to 100% Push The Square" (14×), "I Clicked The Button So Many Times That I Broke The Game" (7.5×). We take MrBeast's **rules** (rule 4 below — a MUST for every title), not his shapes. A MrBeast shape ("X vs Y", "Every…") may be a **B swap title** as a test, never the live A title.

## The 7 Brimlo patterns

| # | Pattern | Real examples (views, theirs) | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **I [did] [BIG NUMBER] [thing] [short twist]** | I can't leave until I pick up 1,000,000 coins (200K) · I got paid to destroy 100 quintillion dollars (274K) · I Pressed This Button 1 MILLION Times! (4.8×, 2.8K subs) | A true number of **1,000,000+** on screen. **Default for simple games** |
| **P2** | **I [did a crazy thing] [funny result]** / **I [did X] Until I [funny result]** | i flipped bottles until i lost my mind · I made the grim reaper look unemployed (604K) · I printed so much money I scared the king (408K) | Something in the game reacts, breaks or gets absurd. **Default for complex games** |
| **P3** | **They Made Me [huge job]** / **They Gave Me [tiny tool] and Told Me to [huge job]** | they made me sort the entire ocean (123K) · They Gave Me A Toothbrush And Told Me To Clean The Moon (126K) | Job-sim, cleaning, sorting: a boss gives you the work |
| **P4** | **This [Thing] Isn't Going to [Verb] Itself** | this lake isn't going to empty itself (316K) | One huge boring job: dig, empty, peel, clean |
| **P5** | **I Can't Leave Until I [goal]** / **I Had to [goal] Before [threat]** | I had to spend $1 billion before the tax man took it (248K) | The game has a clear finish or a timer |
| **P6** | **A Game Where Being [X] Gets You [Y]** | a game where being adorable gets you canned (192K) | The game has an ironic rule |
| **P7** | **You Must [Verb] the [Thing]** / **The [Thing] Must Be Fed** | the fire must be fed (100K) · you must roll 3 in a row (137K) | One strange command drives the whole game |

Rare extra (max 1 in 10 videos): "This Is the Most Satisfying Game of the Year" — only if you really mean it (see "Satisfying" below).

## Rules

0. **Title A = the Brimlo house pattern** (27 Nov 2026, [four-channel study](../research/reports/2026-11-27-four-channel-study/README.md)): **"I [did the chore] [100% of] [GIANT THING] With [tiny tool]"** — our two best videos: "I Drank 100% of the OCEAN With a Straw" (3,991), "I Emptied 100% of a DESERT With a Spoon" (1,974). Each growing channel has one fixed style people recognize (IdleAlex "I Get Paid to…", LuckyLemon "…7,455,642 Times…"). Use "100%" only when it is true; else "I [did the chore] a GIANT [thing] With [tiny tool]". If the game has no tiny tool, use rule 6 (bold joke) for A. B and C always use other patterns.
1. **No game name in titles** (27 Nov 2026; replaces "decide by game type"). The four growing channels used it in 4 of 119 videos, and Drink the Ocean got 78% search traffic without it. The name always goes in the first description line, tags and chapters. One exception stays: an **old video (past ~7 days) that ranks top 3 in search for the game name** keeps " - Game Name" at the end when its title is swapped.
2. **Never "Demo" or "incremental" in the title.** "100%" only if you really finished it.
3. **Title Case**, not all lowercase (that is Azrilich's look). Max **one** CAPS word.
4. **MUST — the 3 MrBeast checks** (Mamunur, Thu 19 Nov 2026; every A, B and C title passes all 3):
   - **A true big number — at least 1,000,000** — from the video, in digits ("1,000,000 Blocks", "1 MILLION Times", "100 Quintillion Dollars", not "a million"). **If the biggest true number in the video is under 1,000,000** (e.g. 7,066 trees, 3,000 ants), this check does **not** apply (Mamunur, Thu 19 Nov 2026): the title does not need a number, and the clear goal or result carries it. A small number may still be used if it helps the story, but it does not count as the "big number".
   - **One clear goal or result** a stranger understands in one second ("…to Find One Golden Ticket", "…to Feed a Giant Worm").
   - **Short:** 30–55 characters, 5–9 words (a " - Game Name" tail may go up to ~70).
5. **First person past tense** ("I fed…", "I sorted…") in most titles. Do not use the same pattern 3 videos in a row.
6. **Bold joke title first, not a report** (Mamunur, Fri 27 Nov 2026 — replaces the old "true to the video" rule). Title A is a **bold, funny, radical sentence** like Azrilich's ("I kicked a rock so hard it broke reality", "this game literally paid me to cheat" 2.7×, "they gave me the worst tool for this job" 2.5×). It sells a feeling or a joke. It does **not** need to match what he says in the video word for word, and it does not need to list what happened.
   - **Build it from the game's core idea** (its theme, main action or the big result): Monopoly + a sword, a rock that gets kicked, an ocean to drink. Stretch it into a joke: "until I became LEGENDARY", "broke reality", "scared the king".
   - **The only line:** the viewer must get what the title promises — the game, the action and the feeling are really in the video. Don't promise a specific thing the viewer clicks for that never shows up (a 1,000,000 number that never comes, a boss win that did not happen, "100%" when you stopped early). Those make people leave fast, and that hurts the video.
   - A tool score never decides alone. A bold joke title with a lower score is fine.
7. **Own words:** never copy a title. Copy the *shape*. (SB Atomic used Ash Swoon's exact title → 118 views.)
8. **Funny is good, mean is not.** A small twist from this game's real events ("…And I Regret Nothing", "…Before It Ate Me").
9. **Hook first:** the first ~40 characters must make a stranger curious without knowing the game.
10. Thumbnail text never repeats the title (see thumbnails.md). No emoji, no creator names, simple English he can say.

## Game name: decide by game type — REPLACED 27 Nov 2026 by rule 1 (no game name). Kept for history.

Market check Tue 10 Nov 2026 (24 games). Data: [game-name report](../research/reports/2026-11-10-game-name-in-title-check.md).

- **Survivor / horde / zombie / roguelite / action, or launch day (0–2 days) of a hyped game:** name **allowed at the end** (" \| Game Name" or " - Game Name") or inside the sentence. People search these by name.
- **Everything else (cozy, satisfying, sim, clicker, cleaning):** story title, **no name**. Our cozy videos got the same small search traffic with or without it.
- **Never the game name first:** 0 wins in 24 games.
- **Old video with a proven name search** (Wed 11 Nov 2026): when you swap the title of a video past its first ~7 days that ranks top 3 in YouTube search for the game name, or gets a clear share of views from Search, **keep " - Game Name" at the end**, any game type. Example: "I Scraped a Dinosaur Skull Clean With My Fingernail - Scrapecremental".
- Shape alone does not make a hit. Timing (launch wave) and thumbnail matter more.

## Title recipe by game type

**Every title starts here.** Fill this card (from the video file, Steam page or recording):

```
Game name:        ...
Game type:        (one row from the table below)
Concept (1 line): I [main action] to [goal]
Hot right now?:   launch/playtest ≤ 48 h, or YouTube wave started ≤ 48 h ago? yes/no
Core fact:        biggest TRUE number · tiny tool vs huge job · end result · funniest moment
```

| Game type (how to know it) | Name in title? | Hook that wins | Real winning titles (channel subs → views) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Survivor / horde / zombie / tower defense / roguelite / action** | **Yes, at the end** | **1 me vs HUGE NUMBER of enemies** · "This is a … About …!" · "How long can I survive" (P1, P5) | Ash Swoon 3.6K → 41K "This is an Incremental Game About Holding Off Climbing Zombies! \| Wait, They Can Climb?!" · Erdem 42K → 64K "1 vs 10,000 Zombies - How Many Days Can I Survive?" · Sharkinbear 2.3K → 18.6K "The Only Thing Your Rats Remember Is The Taste Of Flesh And Bone - I Am Plague" |
| **Cleaning / restoring / tidy-up job** | No | **Tiny tool vs huge dirty place** (P3, P4) | FungalBrainRot 8.4K → 129K "They Gave Me A Toothbrush And Told Me To Clean The Moon" · IdleAlex 629 → 52K "I Have to Clean Up a Bank Buried in Cash" · Mon EvenStar 25K → 63K "I'm Getting Paid to Drain a Lake…" · ⚠ plain "Tidy Up Game about…" got 100–650 |
| **Big-number collect / clicker** | No | **"I Had To [verb] [ABSURD NUMBER] [thing] To [goal]"** (P1, P5) | DangerouslyFunny → 767K "I Had To Collect A Million Coins To Find The Bottom" · mcveety 34K → 150K "I Have To Harvest A Quadrillion Carrots" · Ridiculously Random 1.6K → 78K "I Didn't Know Numbers Went This High..." |

If a game fits two rows, use the row of its **main loop** (what you do 80% of the time). Not sure → the no-name story title is the safe default.

## How to make a title (step by step)

1. Fill the card above and pick the **game type row**.
2. Write the core fact in one line.
3. **A (live title)** = the row's winning hook, our own words, the core fact. Name only if the row says so.
4. **B** = a different pattern (P1–P7) of the same row. **C** = the name version if the row allows a name; otherwise a third pattern.
5. Check against the **Rules** (true, **the 3 MUST checks: true number of 1,000,000+ (only if the video has one), one clear goal, short**, Title Case, hook first, not the same pattern as the last 2 videos, never the name first, not a copy).
6. Score with `brimlo_score_title` (and vidIQ if useful) **only to compare** — the truth and the row win over a score.

## A/B/C title set (every video) + hand swaps

**Always give 3 titles as an A/B/C set** (Mamunur, 6 Nov 2026). **Since 10 Nov 2026 B and C are swap titles, not a Studio A/B test:**

```
A (live): …
B (swap): …
C (swap): …
```
- A = the best pick; the video goes live with it.
- **Each one a different pattern** (P1–P7), so a swap really tries a new idea.
- All 3 true to the video and follow the Rules. One line under each: the pattern + why.

**Hand swap:**
1. Go live with A. Do not start a Studio A/B test.
2. After 2–3 days, check views and impressions CTR in Studio. If the video is below the channel median (or CTR is clearly low), change to B by hand.
3. Write the swap date and old/new title in the video file and in the Title test log below. Wait 2–3 more days before judging B. Swap to C only if B is also weak.

**Why no Studio A/B test now (10 Nov 2026):** YouTube says the most common reason for "Inconclusive" is too few impressions; guides say ~1,000–5,000 impressions per title are needed. Our videos get ~30–1,000 views. Sources: [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en), [Gyre](https://gyre.pro/blog/youtubes-new-title-ab-testing-tool-everything-creators-need-to-know), [Tintin Smith](https://youtubehowto.substack.com/p/is-ab-testing-useful).
**Use Studio A/B tests again** on a video with ~1,000+ impressions a week, older videos first. Make the titles very different and do not touch the title during the test.

## "Satisfying" (13 Oct 2026)

It is a **browse/feeling word, not a search keyword** (vidIQ). Mild evidence: Idle Cub's 6 "satisfying" titles median 439K vs 282K for the rest (small, biased sample).
1. Use it only when the screen is smooth, repeating progress (crush, mine, clean, sort, automate). Skip it for dark, gambling, challenge and story games.
2. Put it **after a concrete idea**, never alone. At most **1 video in 3**.

## Shorts title system (Mon 16 Nov 2026) — PAUSED

**Shorts are stopped (18 Nov 2026, see `plan/roadmap.md`).** Use this only if Mamunur restarts them. The long-video rules above do not apply to Shorts.

Why different: in the Shorts feed the picture plays first and the title is a small line at the bottom. It must match the first 2 seconds, be short, and carry the game name as a hashtag. Data (16 Nov 2026, ~230 Shorts): winners 25–45 characters, questions and "This Game…" lines common, 1–2 CAPS words, game name in a hashtag not the sentence. Examples: "Clean the Moon with a TOOTHBRUSH #gaming" (290K) · "This Game Takes 400 Real Days to Beat" (134K, 493 subs) · "Why Am I Addicted to Kicking Rocks?" (585K).

| # | Shape | Brimlo example | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S1** | **Question** — "How Big Is This…?!", "Why Am I Addicted to…?", "Can You…?" | Can One Tiny Mushroom Cover the WHOLE Earth? | The Short has a clear answer at the end |
| **S2** | **"This Game…"** — Makes You [odd job] / Takes [big number] / Lets You [crazy thing] | This Game Makes You Drink the WHOLE Ocean | The game's idea is strange or funny on its own |
| **S3** | **Tiny tool vs huge job / big number**, as a command or short fact | Drink the OCEAN With One Straw | One tool, one huge job, or one huge number |
| **S4** | **"I" story, short** | I Waited the Whole Game for This MINIGUN | A personal moment |

Rules: A/B/C set uses 3 different shapes · 25–45 characters before the hashtags, whole title under 75 · first words = the first 2 seconds · 1–2 CAPS words · no game name in the sentence (only a famous, searched game) · **exactly 2 hashtags at the end: `#GameNameNoSpaces #GenreTag`** (`#IncrementalGame`, `#IdleGame`, `#SurvivorLike` or `#ZombieGame`; never `#shorts`) · true to the Short, no "Demo", no emoji, no creator names · questions must have a real answer in the Short.
