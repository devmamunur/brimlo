## Brimlo title system (Mamunur, Fri 6 Nov 2026) — USE THIS

**This replaces every older rule and template in this file** (the old ones are kept below only for history). Built from Azrilich (50 titles) and LuckyLemon (14 titles), checked 5 Nov 2026. ### What they do (facts)

| | Azrilich (50 titles, median 92K views) | LuckyLemon (14 titles) |
|---|---|---|
| Game name in title | **0 of 50** | 0 of 14 |
| "incremental", "\|", "Demo" | never | never |
| Length | median **39 characters, 7 words**, max 61 | ~45 characters |

The title is a **tiny story or a joke about what happened**, not a description of the game.

### The 7 Brimlo patterns

| # | Pattern | Real examples (views, theirs) | Use when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **I [did] [BIG NUMBER] [thing] [short twist]** | I can't leave until I pick up 1,000,000 coins (200K) · I got paid to destroy 100 quintillion dollars (274K) · I Farmed 1,000,000,000,000,000 Carrots And I Regret Nothing (45K) | A true big number on screen. **Default for simple games** |
| **P2** | **I [did a crazy thing] [funny result]** / **I [did X] Until I [funny result]** | i flipped bottles until i lost my mind · I scratched lottery tickets until I won · I made the grim reaper look unemployed (604K) · I printed so much money I scared the king (408K) · I sacrificed an entire frog civilization to a hole (187K) | Something in the game reacts, breaks or gets absurd. **Default for complex games** |
| **P3** | **They Made Me [huge job]** / **They Gave Me [tiny tool] and Told Me to [huge job]** | they made me sort the entire ocean (123K) · They Gave Me A Toothbrush And Told Me To Clean The Moon (126K, FungalBrainRot) | Job-sim, cleaning, sorting: a boss gives you the work |
| **P4** | **This [Thing] Isn't Going to [Verb] Itself** | this lake isn't going to empty itself (316K) · these potatoes aren't gonna peel themselves | One huge boring job: dig, empty, peel, clean |
| **P5** | **I Can't Leave Until I [goal]** / **I Had to [goal] Before [threat]** | I had to spend $1 billion before the tax man took it (248K) | The game has a clear finish or a timer |
| **P6** | **A Game Where Being [X] Gets You [Y]** | a game where being adorable gets you canned (192K) · a game where being useless gets you promoted (76K) | The game has an ironic rule |
| **P7** | **You Must [Verb] the [Thing]** / **The [Thing] Must Be Fed** | the fire must be fed (100K) · you must roll 3 in a row (137K) | One strange command drives the whole game (feed, roll, grow) |

**Azrilich tests titles:** on 6 Nov his titles on Mamunur's screen were different from the public API (e.g. "just one more bottle flip…" → "i flipped bottles until i lost my mind", "I dug too deep on mars" → "they shouldn't have let me dig on mars"). Use Studio **Test & Compare** for titles too when it is available.

Rare extra (max 1 in 10 videos): "This Is the Most Satisfying Game of the Year" (225K) — only if you really mean it.

### Brimlo rules (our own look, not a copy)

1. **Never the game name in the title.** It goes in the first description line + tags.
2. **Never "Demo", "incremental", "|" or "100%"** (unless you really finished the full game).
3. **Title Case**, not all lowercase (lowercase is Azrilich's signature). **No CAPS words**, or max one.
4. **Short:** aim for **30–55 characters, 5–9 words.**
5. **First person past tense** ("I fed…", "I sorted…") in most titles. Use P3–P7 for variety; do not use the same pattern 3 videos in a row.
6. **True to the video:** every number and claim must happen on screen. Write the final title **after** recording (or check it after). A score never beats the truth.
7. **Own words:** never copy a title from another channel. Copy the *shape*.
8. **Funny is good, mean is not.** A small joke or twist ("And I Regret Nothing", "…Before It Ate Me", "…and the Cats Were Not Happy") from this game's real events.
9. **Thumbnail text never repeats the title** (see thumbnails.md).

### How to make a title (step by step)

1. Write in one line what really happened in the video (the biggest number, the funniest moment, the end result).
2. Pick 2–3 patterns that fit (P1/P2 first).
3. Write 5 titles, each under ~55 characters.
4. Read each out loud: would a stranger who never heard of the game click it? If it needs the game name to make sense, rewrite it.
5. Check: true? short? no game name? not the same pattern as the last 2 videos?
6. Score with `brimlo_score_title` (and vidIQ if useful) **only to compare** — the truth and the pattern win over a score.

### A/B/C test set (every video)

**Always give 3 titles as an A/B/C test set** (Mamunur, 6 Nov 2026) — ready to paste into YouTube Studio → title → A/B Testing (Test & Compare):

```
A (main): …
B: …
C: …
```
- A = the best pick; it is the title the video goes live with. B and C are the test titles.
- **Each one a different pattern** (P1–P7), so the test tells us which *shape* wins, not 3 copies of one idea.
- All 3 true to the video, all follow the rules above, each under 100 characters (YouTube limit), aim 30–55.
- One line under each: the pattern + why.

After about 7 days, open Studio → video → "View test report" and log the winner in the Title test log below.

## OLD rules and templates (before 6 Nov 2026) — history only, do not use

### Two title shapes (6 Nov 2026, first version — replaced the same day by the system above)

**This rule wins over rules 4–5 and templates F and J below.** Why: we checked 6 small channels on 5 Nov. None of the growing ones uses a long story + "| Game Name". On the same game, same day, LuckyLemon got 44,796 views on Quadrillion Carrots with no game name, and we got 85. Study: [research/reports/2026-11-06-small-channels-gap-check.md](../research/reports/2026-11-06-small-channels-gap-check.md).

| Game type | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple game** (one object, a number grows) | **I [did] [BIG NUMBER in digits] [thing] [short twist]** | I Sorted 1,000,000 Fish · I Mined 1,000,000 Rocks With One Pickaxe |
| **Complex game** (colony, cards, bosses, many systems) | **[Cool goal] in this [theme] incremental game** | Feed a giant worm so it won't eat your ants in this ant colony incremental game |

- Game name: first line of the description + tags. Not in the title.
- Short: aim for **under ~60 characters** for shape 1.
- The number and the claim must be **true in the video** (check after recording, not before).
- Test: the next 3 videos (from Sun 8 Nov 2026). Review CTR + views after 7 days each. If it is clearly worse, go back.

### Old rules

1. Put **"incremental"** in most titles. ("incremental game" searches +71%; "idle games" −54%.)
2. One clear promise: **tiny → huge**, a strange idea, or a feeling.
3. 1–3 words in CAPS. No emoji. Simple English.
4. **Pure incremental** → sell the idea, put the game name in the description.
5. **Hyped launch / Next Fest demo / playtest / roguelite / tower defense** → put the game name in the title ("| Game Name" at the end, or inside the sentence). **Do not write "Demo" in the title** (Mamunur, 26 Oct 2026: it looks cheap, and it gets old when the full game comes out). Put "demo" in the description and tags instead. If the title says "100%", make it true without the word: "before release", or name the real goal (the boss, the max level).
6. Never put other creators' names in titles or tags.
7. Don't copy another channel's exact words. Copy the *shape*.
8. Write 3 options for every video. Test with Test & Compare if your Studio has it.

## "Satisfying" decision (13 Oct 2026)

**Evidence (mild, not proof):**
- Idle Cub top-50: 6 titles say "satisfying" → average **409K** views (median 439K) vs 44 other titles → average **344K** (median 282K). Small sample; the list is his *top* videos, so it is biased toward winners.
- Azrilich: "most satisfying cleaning game ever" 162K (above his usual), "shoot block = max satisfaction" 54K (below).
- vidIQ search: "satisfying game" is mostly Roblox, Minecraft and tutorial searches → it is a **browse/feeling word, not a search keyword**.

**Rules:**
1. Use "satisfying" only when the screen is smooth, repeating progress (crush, mine, clean, sort, automate).
2. Skip it for dark/horror, gambling, challenge/cheat, story and calm/relaxing games — use the matching feeling word (DISTURBING, BROKEN, RELAXING…).
3. Put it **after a concrete idea**, never alone.
4. At most **1 video in 3**. Overuse makes titles look the same.
5. Test: after 10 videos, compare "satisfying" titles vs others (CTR and 7-day views) in the log below.
