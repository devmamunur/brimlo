# Phrase bank

Simple English sentences for live commentary. Keep this open on a second screen or print it. Practice 5 new lines each week. Over time you will stop translating in your head.

## Starting

- "Okay, let's start."
- "So this is [game]. In this game, you [idea]."
- "I start with only [one / a few] [thing]."
- "The goal is simple: [grow / dig deeper / earn more]."
- "Let's look at the upgrades first."

## Reading and choosing upgrades

- "Let's read this one…"
- "This upgrade gives [2x / +10%] [money / speed]."
- "It costs [500]. I have [300]. So I need to wait a bit."
- "I think this is the best one right now."
- "I'll buy this one, because [it makes more money / it's faster]."
- "Hmm, I'm not sure about this one. Let's try it."
- "This one is expensive, but I think it's worth it."
- "I'll save my money for the big one."

## Numbers going up

- "Before, I made [10] per second. Now I make [1,000]."
- "That's a big jump."
- "We just passed one million!"
- "The numbers are getting crazy now."
- "Look at that number."
- "This is growing very fast."

## Surprise and feelings

- "Oh, wow. I didn't expect that."
- "Okay, this is really satisfying."
- "This is getting out of hand."
- "That was a mistake. Let's fix it."
- "Hmm. That didn't work."
- "Oh no… okay, it's fine."
- "Now we're getting somewhere."
- "I like this a lot."

## Explaining the game

- "So how does this work? When you [do X], you get [Y]."
- "This is a new resource. It's used for [Z]."
- "Prestige means you reset, but you get a bonus. The next run is faster."
- "This part is important, so let me explain."

## Waiting and speed-up

- "This part is slow, so let's speed it up."
- "Now we wait a bit for the money."
- "Let's skip ahead a few minutes."
- "While this runs, let me tell you my plan."

## Planning out loud

- "Next, I want to unlock [X]."
- "My plan is: first [X], then [Y]."
- "I think I need more [money / power] before the next part."
- "Let's try a different strategy."

## Opinions

- "The best part of this game is [X]."
- "One thing I don't like is [Y]."
- "It's simple, but it's very addictive."
- "For a demo, this is really good."
- "I think the full game will be great."

## Ending

- "So that's it for today."
- "I really [liked / didn't like] this game."
- "If you like incremental games, try this one."
- "It's [free / $price] on Steam. The link is in the description."
- "Thanks for watching. See you in the next video."

## Fixing yourself (it's normal)

- "Sorry — let me say that again."
- "What I mean is…"
- "Wait, no. I mean [X]."

## Practice routine (10 minutes, Thursday)

1. Read 5 lines from one section out loud, 3 times each.
2. Record yourself on your phone saying the opening of this weekend's video.
3. Listen once. Fix one thing. Record again.
