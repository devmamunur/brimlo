# Phrase bank

Short English sentences for live commentary in any game. Print it. Say them out loud. Over time, you will stop translating in your head.

Part 1 comes from counting the most used phrases in 13 Azrilich videos (31 Oct 2026). The sentences and jokes are written for Brimlo, in a calm voice. Fill `[ ]` with what is on your screen.

**You don't need to talk fast. Say it calmly. Small mistakes are normal.**

## Practice plan: 10 minutes a day

| Day | Practice |
|---|---|
| Monday | Part 1 + Part 2 |
| Tuesday | Part 3 + Part 4 |
| Wednesday | Part 5 + Part 6 |
| Thursday | Part 7 + Part 8 |
| Friday | Part 9 + Part 10 |
| Saturday | Part 11 + Part 12 |
| Sunday | Your 10 favorite lines |

1. Read each line out loud 3 times.
2. Pick 5 lines. Say them without looking at the paper.
3. Before recording, choose 3 lines and use them in the video.
4. Tick a line when you can say it without thinking.

## Part 12: Starting and ending

**Starting. Say it while you are already playing:**

- So, [strange thing that happened to me].
- Now I have to [the game's job].
- I start with only [one] [thing].
- The goal is simple: [get all of them].
- You rest. I'll [the game's job].
- Okay, let's start.

**Don't say these in the first 2 minutes:** "Hello, welcome" · "Today I'm going to…" followed by a tour · "What is this?" about a menu · reading upgrade or story text.

**Ending:**

- So that's it for this game.
- The best part is [X]. The weak part is [Y].
- If you like [X], try this one.
- It's free on Steam. The link is in the description.
- Thanks for watching.
- You've done enough today. Rest well.
- You rest. I'll keep going.
- See you in the next one.
