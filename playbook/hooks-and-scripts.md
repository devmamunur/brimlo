# Hooks and recording scripts

You talk live. A script is **not** something to read word-for-word. It is a **card** with the opening, a few milestone lines, and the ending — practiced 2–3 times before you press record. Everything in the middle is natural talk.

Use `/script [game]` to make a card for a specific game. More ready-made sentences: [phrase-bank.md](phrase-bank.md).

## Video structure (60–120 min)

| Part | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold open** | 0:00–0:15 | 5–15 s of the best late-game moment. No logo, no intro. |
| **Hook line** | 0:15–0:30 | One promise: "This started with X. [time] later… this. Let's see how." |
| **The idea** | 0:30–1:00 | The game in one sentence + why it's interesting (new demo, strange idea). |
| **Early game** | 1–10 min | Explain the first upgrades simply. Viewers decide in the first ~5 minutes. |
| **Middle** | 10 min → end | Milestones as chapters. Talk at every decision. Speed up waits. |
| **Ending** | last 1–2 min | Honest verdict + who it's for + price/demo + "see you in the next video". End screen. |

## Hook formulas (first 30 seconds)

Pick one. Fill the brackets.

1. **Tiny → huge:** "This started with [one rat / one coin / one rock]. [Two hours] later… this. Let's see how it happened."
2. **Question:** "Can [one tiny thing] really become [huge thing]? Let's find out."
3. **Strange idea:** "In this game, you [strange idea]. And it gets out of hand very fast."
4. **New / early:** "This game isn't even out yet. The [demo / playtest] is free right now, and it's [surprising feeling]."
5. **100% run:** "I played this game until I unlocked everything. Here's the whole journey, from zero to 100%."
6. **Honest opinion:** "I didn't expect much from this game. I was wrong."

## Ending formulas

1. **Verdict:** "So that's [game]. The best part is [X]. The weak part is [Y]. I'd give it [score/10]."
2. **Who it's for:** "If you like games where you start tiny and end huge, this one is for you."
3. **Price / demo:** "It's [$price / free as a demo] on Steam. Link in the description."
4. **Close:** "Thanks for watching. If you want more games like this, subscribe. See you in the next one."

Keep the ending short. Put an **end screen** to your most similar video.

## Commentary rules

- **Say what you see.** Read upgrade text out loud. That's easy English and useful for viewers.
- **Say why.** "I buy this because it doubles my income."
- **Compare numbers.** "Before: 10 per second. Now: 1,000 per second."
- **Silence is OK for a few seconds.** If you have nothing to say, speed it up later.
- **Mistake in a sentence?** Pause 2 seconds, say it again. Easy to cut.
- **Don't fake excitement.** Calm and honest is your brand.
- **Press the marker hotkey** at every milestone — these become chapters.

## Milestone moments to talk about

First upgrade · first automation · first new resource · first prestige/reset · a number crossing a big line (1K, 1M, 1B) · a new area/floor · a surprise · a mistake · the final unlock.
