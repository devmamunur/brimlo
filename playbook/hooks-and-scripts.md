# Hooks and recording scripts

You talk live. A script is **not** something to read word for word. It is a **card** with the start, the lines for big moments and the ending. Practice it 2–3 times, then press record. Everything in the middle is natural talk.

`/script [game]` makes the card ([script-card/template.md](script-card/template.md)). Ready-made sentences: [phrase-bank.md](phrase-bank.md).

**The data behind these rules:**
- [First 5 minutes study](../research/reports/2026-11-19-first-5-minutes/README.md) (Thu 19 Nov 2026): 10 channels, 50 videos, with all transcripts.
- Our own retention: [head-to-head review](../research/reports/2026-10-28-head-to-head-review.md) (28 Oct 2026), and Drink the Ocean (Nov 2026): 96% still watching at ~1:00, only **14% at 2:00**.

## Video structure (60–120 min)

| Part | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Start: Reason → Goal → Go** | 0:00–0:15 | 3 lines, written before recording (below). The first action starts while you say line 2. |
| **First loop** | 0:15–1:30 | Do the main action → say the first number → buy the **first upgrade before 1:30** → test it. |
| **The loop** | 1:30 → end | do → number → choose upgrade + why → buy → test → before/after → remind the goal. A short reaction every 20–30 s. |
| **Subscribe line** | after the first big upgrade (~5–10 min) | **Never in the first 5 min** (only 2 of 50 niche videos do it). One short line tied to the story: "Want to see how big I get? Subscribe and stay with me." No upload-plan sentence. |
| **Middle** | 10 min → end | Milestones become chapters (press the marker hotkey). Speed up waits. |
| **Ending** | last 1–2 min | 4 warm lines (below). End screen. |

**Cold open:** optional, and **none of the 50 study videos use one**. Only use `/hook` mode A (a 2 s gold text pop over your first line) if you want. The "2-moment preview" test was dropped on 19 Nov 2026: no top niche video uses a preview.

## The start: Reason → Goal → Go (most important rule)

How our niche starts now (19 Nov 2026): FonFon, mcveety and Azrilich use it in 15 of 15 videos. In our niche, videos that start this way have a median of ~147K views. Videos that start with "we're playing a game called…" have ~26K. (Bigger channels use this start, so it is not the only reason, but it's clear what works.)

**Line 1 — The reason.** Something silly happened to *me*. Short and a bit funny. It doesn't need to be true.
**Line 2 — "So…" + the goal with a number.** Start the first action while you say it.
**Line 3 — The journey + go.** Tiny tool now, big thing later. "Let's get [verb]-ing."

> FonFon (2.7M views): "I was locked in a room with a giant stack of hay. So, to escape, I'm going to have to… build a fully automated factory just to find a single needle. Let's get sifting."
> mcveety (540K, Drink the Ocean): "Someone's drowning at the bottom of this ocean. So to save them, I just need to…" (already drinking)

**Reason ideas** (fill in the game; a new one every video):
- "I was locked in [a room / a cell / a farm] with [huge pile]."
- "My grandpa left me [a shop / a farm / a mine]. It's a mess."
- "My boss gave me one [straw / toothbrush / fingernail] and said: clean all of it."
- "I lost my [phone / key] in [the ocean / this field]."
- "Everyone laughed at my tiny [farm / army / shop]."
- "A [tornado / storm / alien] destroyed my [farm / town]."
- "A rich man called me. For every [dollar / tree] I [burn / cut], he pays me."

**No story fits?** Use the backup: **goal + number**, while you already play. "There are 1,000,000 coins here. I'm going to pick up every single one." (LuckyLemon and Ridiculously Random start like this: median ~63K.)

**Never in the first 2 minutes:**
- "Hello, welcome" or "This is the game named…" (the title already says it)
- Saying the goal twice
- Reading the store page, help text or story text
- A map, menu or room tour
- "Maybe, maybe… what is this?" with no action. Say "Let's try it" and try it.
- Settings or language screens: do these before OBS, then start a **new save**.

"Today I'm going to…" is fine if the action starts right after it. Reading **one** upgrade name and buying it right away is fine.

## The loop (0:15 → end)

The same loop in the top videos (Azrilich 663K, mcveety 540K, FonFon 2.7M):

1. **Do** the action and talk while you do it.
2. **Say the number:** "$48. Nice." / "That's 0.1% done." / "I'm up to 200."
3. **Choose the next upgrade and say why:** "I want this one. It makes me faster."
4. **Buy it and test it right away.**
5. **Before and after:** "Before: 5 at a time. Now: 8. Much better."
6. **Remind the big goal:** "Still a long way to go." / "13 million to go."
7. **A small reaction every 20–30 s:** "Oh no." / "Wait, what?" / "Yes!" / "Okay, okay."

**Talk more often, not faster.** Niche channels talk at 180–250 words/min. We talk at 127–155, the same as Wanderbots and Malakye G, who do fine. Don't speed up; with English as a second language it sounds forced. Fill silence with short lines instead. Silence longer than ~3 s gets cut in the edit.

## Commentary rules

- **Say the upgrade short.** "Double damage. Buying it." Never read a whole menu.
- **Keep the score out loud** every few minutes. "2 million now. Next goal: 1 billion."
- **Stay positive when it's slow.** "Okay. Slowly. We'll get there." Never complain.
- **Skip the grind out loud.** "Let me skip ahead a bit." Then speed it up in the edit.
- **One running joke per video, new every time.** It comes from this game (its rules, its enemy, its theme). Never reuse one (Mamunur, 1 Nov 2026).
- **"Not yet" promises:** "Hey, a gold door. I can't open it. …Not yet." Pay it off later: "Remember that door? Watch this."
- **Mistake in a sentence?** Pause 2 seconds and say it again. It's easy to cut.
- **Calm and honest.** Small real reactions, never fake hype.
- **Press the marker hotkey** at every milestone. These become chapters.

**Milestones to talk about:** first upgrade · first automation · first new resource · first prestige/reset · a number crossing a big line (1K, 1M, 1B) · a new area · a surprise · a mistake · the final unlock.

## Ending (last 1–2 min)

4 warm lines, good part only (Mamunur, 6 Nov 2026):

1. "And that's it! I was just [tiny start]. And look at me now. [Result with numbers]."
2. "My favorite part? [X]."
3. "If you like calm games where something small gets really big, you'll like this one."
4. "Thanks for watching. See you next time."

Don't say: a bad part, a score, the price, "link in the description", "Which game next? Tell me in the comments", or "You rest. I'll keep going."

## Simple words (script language, 6 Nov 2026)

Mamunur is from Bangladesh; English is his second language. Every line must use words he knows and can say without stopping.

- **Talk like telling a story to friends, not giving a presentation:** full casual sentences with "I", "we" and "you". The hero is always "I", with no named character (8 Nov 2026). Small reactions ("Oh no.", "Nice!", "Wait…"), little opinions ("I love it."). No slogan fragments ("Start to end, in one video." ✗), no announcing lines ("In this video we will…" ✗).
- Max ~10–12 words per line. One idea per line.
- **Tense:** now = present ("I'm one tiny mushroom."), later = "will / going to" ("At the end, my roots will be everywhere."), done = past ("I was tiny. Now…").
- Plain words for game actions (**water, food, grow, coins**), not the game's jargon. If a game word is on screen all the time, keep it and explain it once.
- Every card has a **word list with Bangla meanings** and "say it like".
- No idioms, no slang, no jokes that need native timing.

| Don't write | Write |
|---|---|
| somewhere under | under |
| bully / enemy / rival | bad guy / the animals / [name] |
| network / mycelium | roots / "my roots" |
| patience | Okay. Slowly. |
| honestly / actually / basically | (drop it) |
| thirsty | needs water |
| reached | got to |
| evolution tasks / objectives | the tasks / the goal |
| directions | ways |

## How we check it works

On every new video, take **retention at 2:00 and at 5:00** from Studio and compare with Drink the Ocean (14% at 2:00). Write it in the video file's Results.
