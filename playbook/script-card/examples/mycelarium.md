# Example script card — Mycelarium (approved Fri 6 Nov 2026)

A frozen copy of an approved card. **Learn the shape and the English level from it. Never copy its lines, hero name or jokes into another video.** Template: [../template.md](../template.md). Live file: [videos/2026-11-08-mycelarium.md](../../../videos/2026-11-08-mycelarium.md).

Why it is good: a named hero with a small story ("Kevin" the mushroom), a goal with a number in the first 10 seconds, something new every 15–20 seconds in the first 2 minutes, "Not yet" promises paid off later, facts and timings from two real playthrough transcripts, a Bangla word list, very simple English.

## Research block

> You are one tiny mushroom. You grow roots under the ground. The roots drink water and eat food. You grow and grow, until your roots are under the whole forest.

Game facts (from 2 transcripts, read 6 Nov 2026: FungalBrainRot "100%" 116 min [a_SW1Oz8dww], Yusterday 46 min, 31K views [iX-AqoIdHwM] — check on your screen):
- **Grow a root**: left-click and drag. Every root costs **food** (Mamunur played the demo, 6 Nov 2026: roots cost food. The other videos say water — maybe a different version. On recording day, look at which number goes down and say that). **When it rains, growing is free.** (The game calls a root a "hypha shot".)
- **ADD NODE** on a root costs **5 food** (salt). New roots grow from nodes.
- Roots can't go through rock. In clay, roots are shorter. Worms, leaves, bones, flies and later **whole trees** become food.
- More roots = more **upgrade points** (for the upgrade menu).
- "Your mycelium size is your experience": more length = more upgrade points. Each skill costs more (1, then 2…).
- Surface mushrooms can only grow where your roots reach the surface.
- **Evolution tasks** first: drink water, send out spores, make 30 food, build 5 mushrooms, scare off the hedgehog, build 2 mushrooms on trees, build 2 stinky mushrooms, grow 10,000 cm.
- Surface mushrooms: experience, stinky (scares squirrels and crows), predator (eats flies), consumer (eats trees), water, spore, digging (breaks stone), exploding.
- Enemies that eat your mushrooms: **hedgehog, crow, squirrel, boar**.
- **Endings:** find all **5 magic mushrooms** (one is "cosmos", one scares squirrels forever) and/or **the great migration** (a mushroom count, e.g. "40 out of 100"). Deep down you hit the **water table** (water becomes free). You can keep growing after the ending.

## Recording script

Simple words only (rewritten Fri 6 Nov 2026). Short lines. Say them slowly. Small mistakes are fine.

### The story of this video

Kevin is one tiny mushroom. The forest animals want to eat his mushrooms. Kevin is small, but he wants to be big. At the end, Kevin will be under the whole forest. Talk about Kevin like a small friend. Calm and kind.

### Before you press record

1. Check the full game is in your Steam library (the gift was "Pending"). Play it in CrossOver for 10 minutes.
2. Start a **new save**. When it says "Name your mycelium", type **Kevin**. Do this **before** OBS.
3. Set graphics and sound now.
4. Start OBS when the small red mushroom is on screen.
5. **Don't read the help text** on screen ("Eat salt to build nodes…"). Just do it.
6. Don't open the upgrade menu in the first 2 minutes.
7. Take screenshots: the tiny mushroom, the first rain, the first tree eaten, each magic mushroom, the big map at the end.

### Cold open (optional, for `/hook` after the edit — max 10 s)

Show the end: Kevin's roots under the whole forest.

### First 2 minutes (keep moving)

Do something new every 15–20 seconds. Say one short line.

| When | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|

| ~2:00 | Keep growing | Subscribe line (below) |

"Not yet" is a small promise. Later, Kevin eats worms and goes through rock. Then you say "Remember?" (lines below). This keeps people watching.

### Subscribe line (~2:00)

> "Want to see how big Kevin gets? Subscribe and stay with me."

### Running joke: the animals want Kevin's mushrooms

Animals come and eat your mushrooms: hedgehog, crow, squirrel, boar. Kevin is small and nice. The animals are the bad guys. Say only what happens on screen. Use it 4–5 times, not more.

- First hedgehog (at first it only looks): "Look, a hedgehog. He is only looking… for now."
- Hedgehog comes back: "The hedgehog is back. I don't trust him."
- Hedgehog eats a mushroom: "He ate Kevin's mushroom! Kevin will not forget this."
- If a crow eats the mushroom that scares animals: "The crow ate the mushroom that scares crows. Smart crow."
- You scare it away: "Go away, hedgehog! Not today."
- Crow / squirrel / boar: "Another one? Everybody wants Kevin's food."
- Late, when animals can't come close: "Look at that. Now nobody can touch Kevin."

No girlfriend joke. No cat joke.
