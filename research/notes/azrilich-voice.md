## The opening formula

**11 of 13 open with a made-up personal story.** You were right about this. All 13 start with the word "So".

1. **"So," + a strange reason** he is here. One or two short sentences, said as if it is normal.
2. **What he must do now.** The story turns into the game's job.
3. **He plays while talking.** The action starts almost at once, often during the first sentences.

He does **not** say how the video ends. No "this is how I got there". The viewer stays to see what happens.

**Story types:**
- **Trouble, money or punishment (4):** debt, mortgage, speeding, a boss.
- **Family (3):** parents twice, grandpa.
- **Woke up here (3):** strange food, strange drink, an alien.
- **Silly accident (1):** dropped beans.

The story connects to the game's job. It is not random. The title often matches the story too.

## How he talks during the game

- **Speed:** fast, 151–198 words a minute (middle: 180). Mamunur: 75–130 (review, 28 Oct 2026). **Do not copy this speed.**
- **He is not perfect.** He says "um" or "uh" about 0.5–2 times a minute, and he often breaks a sentence and starts again. Small mistakes are fine.
- **Short reaction words, all the time:** "okay" (about 3 times a minute), "oh my gosh", "dude", "boom", "bro". "Perfect" and "beautiful" when something looks good.
- **He talks while he does things:** "if I take this, then this happens". He says what he is doing as he does it.
- **He says what he thinks:** "I guess", "I'm assuming", "wait", "hold on". Being confused is part of the show.
- **Short game text:** he reads small bits aloud while playing, then answers them like a person in a conversation. He does not stop to read long menus.
- **Money out loud:** he says small numbers often ("$2", "that gives us $4").
- **Jokes are deadpan.** The funny part is said calmly as if it is normal. Some jokes are crude.

## What Brimlo should copy

1. **A made-up story in the first 10 seconds**, tied to the game's job.
2. **Starting with "So,".** It sounds natural and is easy to say.
3. **Saying the joke calmly.** This fits the calm Brimlo brand, and it can be written and practiced before recording.
4. **More small reaction words** at your own speed: "okay", "oh", "wait", "nice".
5. **Talking while doing**, so fewer silent parts.

## What Brimlo should not copy

- His speed.
- His exact stories, words or crude jokes. Write our own.
- His short lengths as a rule. Brimlo keeps full-length videos.
- Fake excitement. Calm and honest stays the brand.

## How to write a Brimlo story

> So, [strange thing that happened to me]. [Now I must do the game's job, with a number or a goal.]

Keep it to 2 sentences, about 20 words, and about 10 seconds at Mamunur's speed. Use only simple words.
