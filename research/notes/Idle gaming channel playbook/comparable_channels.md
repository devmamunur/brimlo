# Comparable YouTube Channels to Idle Cub (Incremental / Idle / Clicker / Bullet-Heaven niche), October 2026

Method note for the report writer: Channel metrics come from vidIQ (similar_channels on Idle Cub's channel ID, three channel_search queries, and channel_videos for MOREQUIS, Nerfed Reflexes, CG Grows and Idle Niv). They were pulled on 2026-10-12. vidIQ fields used: subscriberCount, viewCount, videoCount, publishedAt (channel creation), country (where the channel says it is based), subsGrowth30d/1y (percent), longVideoCount30d/1y, longAvgDuration (seconds, converted to minutes below), avgViews, isFaceless. Because vidIQ data has no public URL, each claim cites the channel's YouTube URL (or video URL) as the pointer, with "(vidIQ, 12 Oct 2026)". Percent growth on small bases is noisy. Approximate absolute numbers (e.g., "about +1.4K subs") are my arithmetic from vidIQ percentages and are labelled "≈". Web sources (Reddit, Social Blade, interviews) turned up almost nothing on these specific creators. The one useful web source was topincrementalgames.com's creator directory.

Total vidIQ calls used: 8 (budget respected).

## Q1. Which channels are most similar to Idle Cub in format, and how do they differ?

### Takeaway
The closest format matches are **Dex** (DE, 312K), **MOREQUIS** (US, 62.9K) and **CG Grows** (US, 5.5K, new in June 2026). All three post long (1–2.5h) single-game incremental videos at a high frequency (15–31 per month). **Sifd** (GR, 148K) is an older peer that has stalled. **Nerfed Reflexes** and **Azrilich** cover the same games in shorter 30–45 min edits. The main differences are video length, title formula (100%-completion and huge-number titles vs. curiosity titles vs. descriptive "An incremental game where…" titles) and growth stage.

### Inferences
- **Breakout mechanics.** In every fast-growing channel examined, growth came from 1–3 outlier videos, often 10–40x the channel's median. Examples: CG Grows' parasite video at 551K vs. a typical 10–30K, and Nerfed Reflexes' two videos at about 350K each vs. a typical 10–30K. Volume buys more chances at an outlier.
- **Game selection.** Outliers cluster on brand-new or pre-release Steam incrementals with a visual "swarm/infest/transform/huge number" hook (parasite, germ army, army from nothing to godlike, dead desert restored, $374 quadrillion per second). "Early creator preview" and demo coverage show up among Nerfed Reflexes' top videos, which suggests developer key access matters.
- **Title formula.** Each successful new channel picked one repeatable template and stuck with it (CG Grows: "The X incremental game where Y"; Nerfed Reflexes: "An incremental game where Y"; MOREQUIS: "I 100% … / Turning $0 into $huge"). Putting the genre keyword "incremental game" in the title is common among the new channels and may help search and browse classification. This is an inference, not verified.
- **Length.** Long (1–2h) and medium (30–45 min) videos both work. CG Grows (≈1.5h) and Nerfed Reflexes (≈40 min) both broke out within months.
- CG Grows may be a sister channel of CG Plays (156K), given the similar name, both US and both faceless. If so, it is not a true from-zero channel. This is an inference only and was not verified.

### Gaps
- No direct creator interviews or Reddit posts describing these channels' strategies were found. Web searches for MOREQUIS, Idle Cub and Nerfed Reflexes on Reddit returned nothing relevant.
- Absolute monthly sub gains are computed from vidIQ percentages and may be off by rounding.
- Whether these creators get pre-release keys from developers (and how) is only implied by "Early creator preview" titles and Nerfed Reflexes' "Pitch your game" Discord.

### Gaps

- No calm, non-native incremental channel that *succeeded from scratch in 2025–2026* was identified. Dex, Sifd and Idle Cub all started before 2023.

## Q4. Is the niche saturated? How many channels compete, and are new ones still breaking through?

### Takeaway
The niche is crowded but not closed. Several channels created in 2025–2026 broke through within months: Azrilich (61.7K in about 4 months), Nerfed Reflexes (13K in about 12 months), CG Grows (5.5K in about 3.5 months with a 551K video) and Idle Gamer. At the same time, there is a long tail of new channels with hundreds of uploads and only a few hundred subs. Older incumbents (Sifd, CG Plays) are flat. Roughly 20–25 English channels above about 3K subs publish regularly in this niche.

A newcomer's best opening is (a) very new or pre-release games the big channels have not touched yet, (b) a sharper title angle, or (c) smaller, older or overlooked incrementals.
- The many tiny channels with 100–450 videos and under 300 subs show that volume alone does not work. Packaging (title and thumbnail) and game choice decide outcomes.

## Q5. What upload frequency and length do the growing small channels use?

### Takeaway
Nearly every fast-growing small or new incremental channel posts **4–7 long videos per week** (15–24 per 30 days). Lengths cluster in two bands: **30–45 min** (Azrilich, Nerfed Reflexes, Introvert Party) and **75–120 min** (CG Grows, Idle Gamer, MOREQUIS, Dex). No incremental channel growing at only 1–3 uploads/week was found. The only low-frequency growers were in adjacent niches (Typical Tea, city builders at about 1/week) or used a heavily edited format (Codz, Trash Wizard).

### Inferences
- **Brimlo's plan (2 x 1.5–2h per week) is about one-third of the output of every growing comparable.** On the evidence here, growth would probably be slower than CG Grows or Nerfed Reflexes, because breakouts are hit-driven and volume raises the odds of a hit. Ways to close the gap without more recording time:
  - Cut each 3–4h session into **two videos**: a 30–45 min "highlights" edit (the Azrilich/Nerfed Reflexes band) plus the 1.5–2h full run, or two different games per session.
  - Prioritise brand-new/demo incrementals where competition is thin.
  - Commit to a single, CG Grows-style title template.
- The 1.5–2h length is well supported: CG Grows, Idle Gamer, MOREQUIS and Dex all average 75–120 min and grow. Length is not the constraint. Frequency and game choice are.
- **Best role models for a part-time, non-native, calm creator**, ranked by inference:
  1. **CG Grows**: 1–2h single-game incremental videos, faceless, consistent "The [theme] incremental game where [hook]" titles, focus on new Steam releases. The most copyable format for Brimlo's recording style. (Note the possible link to CG Plays.)
  2. **Nerfed Reflexes**: a 2025 start that reached 13K in a year, "chill" brand, descriptive genre-keyword titles, a "Pitch your game" Discord for dev keys, and replies to every comment. Copy the packaging and the dev-relations playbook. His lengths (30–45 min) suggest Brimlo should also make shorter cuts.
  3. **MOREQUIS**: proves 1.5–2.5h "I 100% …" completion videos at about 3.5/week can triple a channel in a year. "Turning $0 into $[huge number]" titles are a strong outlier pattern.
  4. **Dex / Idle Cub / Sifd**: proof that German/Greek creators speaking English can lead the niche. Model their game selection, not their daily cadence.
  5. **Idle Niv**: cautionary twin. Non-native, 2026 start, about 1h videos. It stalled after drifting into multi-part roguelike series. Avoid series and keep to the incremental core.
  6. **Typical Tea** (adjacent niche): proof that a calm, low-frequency (about 1/week), long-format creator can grow in 2025–2026 if the vibe is distinctive and the community engagement is personal.

### Gaps
- No small incremental channel growing at 1–3 uploads/week was identified. This is the most important missing comparison for Brimlo and may simply not exist in the data sampled.
- Upload-day/time patterns and thumbnail styles were not collected.
- Retention or CTR data is not public for any of these channels.
- Commentary style (talk ratio, pace) is unverified for all channels except where self-described in channel descriptions.
