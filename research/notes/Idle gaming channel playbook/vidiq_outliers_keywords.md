# vidIQ Outliers, Hot Games, Keywords and Title Patterns: Incremental / Idle / Bullet-Heaven / Incremental TD Niche (as of 13 Oct 2026)

Method note for the report writer:
- **vidIQ calls (35 credits total, within budget):**
  - 2 × `vidiq_outliers`. Both used long-form, English titles, channels ≤20,000 subs, published within the last 3 months, 50 results each. Keywords were "incremental game" and "bullet heaven idle clicker tower defense roguelike".
  - 4 × `vidiq_keyword_research`: "incremental game", "bullet heaven", "idle game" and "incremental tower defense" in research mode with country=US, plus "incremental" in matching_terms mode.
  - vidIQ balance went from 115 credits to **80 remaining** (all add-on credits; the renewable bucket is 0 and resets 2026-10-19).
- **Field definitions:**
  - "Outlier score" below is vidIQ's `breakoutScore`, i.e. the multiple of the channel's typical views.
  - Publish dates were converted from vidIQ Unix timestamps (UTC).
  - Length is `videoDuration` in minutes, rounded down.
  - Game names come from the **video tags** vidIQ returned where the title doesn't name the game. Those identifications are marked "(game from tags)".
- **Citations:** vidIQ data has no public URL, so each video claim cites the YouTube video URL as a pointer, "(vidIQ, 13 Oct 2026)". Keyword data cites a YouTube search URL as a pointer. vidIQ keyword metrics are stamped `metricsAsOf 2026-10-12`.
- **Off-niche results excluded:** the outlier pool contained noise that was dropped from the analysis. Examples: Roblox "Say the word" (Sleep Asmr 10M), mobile ad-style gameplay (Android Gameplay KL, Brain Gaming, KDD Gaming, Wa Games), official trailers (Mistfall Hunter, Dungeon Settlers, ALATURKA, Ignite the Depths), 1–8 min speedruns/shorts-length clips (Skirmish "Cookie Clicker in 3 clicks", Waza_Alex "How to Fish" speedrun, Wutz Needle% speedrun), Deltarune fan content.
- **Thumbnails:** 9 outlier thumbnails were downloaded and viewed directly from i.ytimg.com to describe the thumbnail patterns.

## Q1. Which specific videos are breaking out right now (last 30–90 days) on small channels in this niche?

### Takeaway
There are about 35 in-niche long-form outliers from channels under 20K subs in Jul–Oct 2026. The strongest relative breakouts are on **tiny channels (1K–4K subs)** covering a **brand-new or pre-release Steam game with a one-sentence absurd or dark premise**. Examples: Haikrazon (3.72K) hit 144K on FEED THE AI at 1,290x. Jake Unlocked (1.52K) hit 503K on a Needle-in-a-Haystack factory game at 2,786x. Factoristic (1.55K) hit 107K on a "First Look Demo" at 512x. Most of these are 30–100 min long, which fits Brimlo's 1.5–2h format.

### Inferences
- **Small channels can break out.** Several channels under 4K subs got 50x–2,800x their norm in the last 90 days. The very highest multiples are on channels whose typical video gets a few hundred views (e.g. Gaming Ghost at 133 subs, Engineer In Beta at 68). A brand-new Brimlo channel would see similar multiples from even modest hits.
- **Most outlier videos are long.** Of the 40 rows, 30 are 40+ min, and Factoristic (98 min), JustPatrick-Idle (94–95 min), Ash Swoon (112 min) and Fox Clicks Again (83 min) all broke out at Brimlo-like lengths. Length is not a barrier.
- **Timing is first-week.** The biggest multiples went to videos posted within roughly 1–10 days of a game's demo, playtest or viral moment. Examples: Needle videos on 16–23 Sep, I Am Plague videos on 7 Oct, two days into its playtest, and Wanderburg videos on 8–10 Sep, the launch week.

### Gaps
- vidIQ does not return the game name as a field. Game IDs for rows 21, 24, 25 and 26 could not be determined from tags, and row 7 is ambiguous: the tags say "megabonk", but the title and thumbnail suggest an orc-horde incremental. That may be tag spam.
- Channel country is blank for several channels (Haikrazon, BryBry, Sharkinbear, MMSaitama). Whether they are US-based is unknown.
- Outlier scores are vidIQ's proprietary calculation. Very high scores on tiny channels partly reflect a near-zero baseline.

  - Brimlo should filter Next Fest demos by these themes.
- All hot titles are Windows-first indies. Brimlo should budget time to test each in CrossOver before committing a recording slot.

### Inferences

## Q4. Title and thumbnail patterns common among small-channel outliers

### Takeaway
Small-channel outliers mostly **hide the game name** and sell a **one-line premise plus an emotional or extreme payoff**. Common shapes: "A Game Where You [absurd verb] … and It's DISTURBING!", "I Built a Factory to Find ONE Needle", "Will This AI Turn Good Or Evil…?", "The [theme] incremental game where … FIGHTS BACK". Many add a pre-release tag such as "| First Look Demo", "| Playtest" or "| Early creator preview". Roguelike, tower-defense and survivor outliers do the opposite: they name the game after a pipe or dash and lead with a build or achievement claim. Thumbnails are simple, high-contrast and readable at a glance: 2–3 huge words or a number, a before/after or 0%→100% split, or one striking illustrated image.

### Inferences
- **Recommended Brimlo title formula.** These are inferences, best treated as A/B options:
  - (a) "A game where you [premise] … and it [escalates/FIGHTS BACK]"
  - (b) "I [built/grew/turned] [tiny thing] into [HUGE thing/number]"
  - (c) For demos and playtests, append "| First Look Demo" or "| Playtest" during Next Fest.
  - Add the genre word "incremental" somewhere in the title for search. Keep the game name out of the title for pure incrementals, but add it at the end for roguelites/TD with a build hook.
- **Thumbnail recipe:**
  - One idea, max 2–4 words or one number in heavy white/yellow text, OR no text with a before/after or 0%→100% split showing growth.
  - Red/green or dark/bright contrast.
  - Big numbers ("160,000 ORCS") and "growth from tiny to huge" visuals recur because they show the "numbers go up" fantasy at a glance.
  - Two outliers used stylised or AI-looking illustrations rather than gameplay. That can work, but it risks looking clickbait-y if the gameplay doesn't match. Use a gameplay-based approach as the default.
- **Payoff over ownership:** the shared pattern is selling the *premise's emotional payoff* (disturbing, scary, satisfying, broken, insane) rather than the game. This fits a calm commentator: the hook does the work in packaging, and the long video delivers the slow "number go up" journey.

### Gaps
- Only 9 thumbnails were viewed. No CTR data is available for any video.
- No A/B data on game-name-in-title vs. not. The split described above is observational.
- Title scoring tools (vidiq_score_title) were not used, per the budget and no-generation constraints.
