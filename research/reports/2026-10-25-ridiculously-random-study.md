## 2. Title pattern

Every title has the same shape:

**[I + verb] [simple object] [to / until] [huge result]!!!**

- "Flipping Coins Until I'm A BILLIONAIRE!!!"
- "Growing My Hole Until I Swallowed The WORLD!!!"
- "I Made MILLIONS Using Magnets!!!"
- "Making BILLIONS From ONE Pixel?!?!"

Or: **The Most [SATISFYING / ADDICTING] [simple thing] Game!!!**

Rules he follows:
1. **No game name in the title.** The game name is only in the description and tags.
2. 4–8 words. 1–2 words in CAPS (the result: MILLIONS, BILLIONS, MAXED, WORLD).
3. A simple object anyone understands: coins, chickens, leaves, emails, a square, a pixel.
4. A huge result: millions, billions, max level, 100%, "broke the game".
5. Ends with "!!!" or "?!?!". (This is his brand, not a rule for us.)

Why it works (my guess, I can't see his Studio): these titles are made for the **home page and suggested videos**, not for search. A viewer who does not know the game still wants to click.

## 3. Thumbnail pattern

All 25 thumbnails look the same:

1. **Big yellow text at the top**, thick black outline, edge to edge.
2. The text is usually **a number or money** (16 of 25): "$57 BILLION", "999,999,999", "LVL 999", "$1B". Otherwise 2 words: "MAX LVL", "BROKE THE GAME", "TOO SATISFYING!".
3. **One bright game image** under it: a gold pile, a glowing item, a hero. Bright, saturated colors. Not dark.
4. **One red curved arrow** pointing at the hero. On every thumbnail.
5. Sometimes a **split before → after** ("100K → CLEAN", "LVL 1 → MAXED", "100 / 50,000 / 1,000,000"). **2 of his top 3 videos use the split.**

This is very close to our house style (big gold text on top, one before → after image, one arrow). Good news: our style is right. Two differences:
- His pictures are **bright**. Ours are dark navy.
- His text is **a big number** more often.

## 4. Tags and description

**Tags:** 25–35 tags. First the game name in many forms ("unharvest", "unharvest gameplay", "unharvest 100%", "unharvest ending"…), then general words (incremental game, idle game, clicker game, satisfying game, indie games), then his channel name. Some tags have nothing to do with the video ("asmr", "gaming for sleep", "speedrun"). **Do not copy those.** YouTube calls misleading tags spam, and tags help very little anyway.

**Description, always the same order:**
1. One personal line: "subscribe I'm trying to reach 1k :D", "THANKS FOR 500 SUBS - 1k next?"
2. `🎮 Game Name: [Inbox Zero]` + link (itch.io or Steam).
3. Subscribe link.
4. **`CURRENT SUBS: 288`** — he writes his sub count in every description. Viewers feel they are helping him climb.
5. One long keyword paragraph (looks AI-written).
6. 3–6 hashtags.

No chapters.

## 5. How he starts and talks (from 2 transcripts)

- **Second 0, he is already playing.** No "hello, welcome to the channel", no menu tour.
- **The first sentence is the goal with a number:** "So, I have 100,000 unread emails. So, let's go ahead and read them." / "This game is called Quadrillion Carrots… it might take a while to get a quadrillion by picking them by hand."
- He talks all the time. Small jokes. He reacts out loud ("Oh my god", "What the hell was that?").
- He complains honestly (frame rate drops, "this game sucks" for a moment) and keeps going.
- He ends at the moment the game is finished: "That's everything in the demo… if you enjoyed this video, you'll enjoy all the others." No long outro.

Our Quadrillion Carrots video: people who came from impressions watched **only 0:09 on average**. His start gets to the point in one sentence.

## 6. His game choice

- **Tiny, simple, weird games:** clear emails, flip coins, blow leaves, push a square, a loading bar that fights back.
- Many are **free itch.io games** (What The Buck?!, 99%, Push The Square, Toss Office). Others are fresh Steam demos (Dirt Clicker in Next Fest, Unharvest, Quadrillion Carrots).
- **itch.io browser games run on a Mac with no CrossOver.** That is a big free source we don't use.
- Games that big channels skip, so he has no competition on them.

## 7. What Brimlo should copy (and what not)

| # | Copy this | How for Brimlo |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Start in second 0 with the goal + a number** | First line: "I have one knife. In 2 hours I want a minigun." No intro, no story text. |
| 2 | **Titles a stranger wants to click** | Put the result first, game name last: "I Pulled Carrots Until I Had a QUADRILLION \| Quadrillion Carrots Demo". New channel = we still need the game name for search, so keep it at the end. |
| 3 | **Thumbnail: keep the house style, make it brighter** | Bright game colors, not dark navy. Big number on top when you **saw** the number in the video (our rule). One arrow. Before → after split. |
| 4 | **Never stretch a short game** | Long videos stay (Mamunur, 25 Oct 2026). Play until the game is done or stops being fun; don't pad a 40-min game to 2 hours. |
| 5 | **More videos per week** | He does 1 per day. Brimlo: **3–4 long videos per week** (Mamunur, 25 Oct 2026). More videos = more chances for one hit. |
| 6 | **itch.io browser games** | Free, run on Mac, no CrossOver, almost no competition. Add to the scout list. |
| 7 | **Personal line + sub counter in the description** | Top line: "Thanks for watching! Brimlo is at [N] subs, help me get to 100 :)" + "CURRENT SUBS: [N]" (use the real number from Studio). Easy and honest. |
| 8 | **Talk more, react out loud** | Keep the calm Brimlo voice, but react to every upgrade: "Oh, that's fast now!" Never 10 seconds of silence. |

**Don't copy:**
- Unrelated tags ("asmr", "speedrun") and AI keyword paragraphs.
- Daily uploads. You can't keep that up next to a full-time job.
- "!!!" on every title. One "!" or none is fine for Brimlo.
- Numbers on thumbnails you did not see in the game.

## 8. One-line summary

He did not win because of tags. He won because he **made many short videos fast**, about **simple weird games nobody else played**, with **one clear thumbnail style (big number + bright image + red arrow)** and titles a stranger wants to click, and he **starts playing in the first second**. One of those videos hit 47K, and that hit lifted the whole channel.
