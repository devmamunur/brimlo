# Game name in the title? Market check of 24 games (Tue 10 Nov 2026)

**Question:** for our next videos, should the title have the game name, and where?
**Method:** vidIQ YouTube search (`order: viewCount`, long videos, published Sep–Nov 2026) for 24 recent niche games. For each top result I wrote down the title shape, channel size and views. Main focus: **English videos from small channels (under 50K subs)** — our peer group. Views are from 9–10 Nov 2026.

**Games (24):** Wait, They Can Climb?! · Mycelarium · Hay! One Billion! · Way Too Many Fish! · Pack Ripper · Ant Mill Inc · I Am Plague · Outbreak Z · Seabun · Rust & Shine · Quadrillion Carrots · Deforest a Planet · The Big Suck · Frankenmine · Bottle Flip Inc · Outliers: Incrementum · One Million Coins · Crack The Pack · Find The Needle · Company is Watching · Hamstr Protocol · Scrapecremental · 500 Finger Fillet · Slime Splitter

## Title shapes

- **N** = no game name ("We Start With 1 Ant, Then Grow The Colony To Thousands…")
- **E** = hook first, game name at the **end** after " | " or " - " ("This is an Auto-Battler About Surviving Zombie Hordes! | Outbreak Z")
- **S** = game name **inside the sentence** ("I Played 100% Of Mycelarium", "I Get Paid to Deal With Way Too Many Fish")
- **F** = game name **first** ("MYCELARIUM | Managing…")

## Flops (small English, under 1K views) — every shape has them

- N: MuttleyOS 883, Idle Enns 780, War Room RTS 74 (climbing zombies), Gaming Ghost 48
- E: Tabias 485, ShifoPlays 206, **SB Atomic 118 (copied Ash Swoon's title word for word)**, Intu 74, Rdy2Game 59, Fabo 25

So the shape alone does not make a hit. Timing (launch wave), thumbnail and channel matter more. The title shape only helps the right people find and click.

## What the data says (and what it does not)

1. **Game name first (F) = no wins** in 24 games. Never start the title with the game name.
2. **Name at the end (E) wins mostly on survivor / horde / zombie / roguelite games:** 5 of the 6 E hits (Ash Swoon ×3, BryBry, Sharkinbear). These games get searched by name (playtests, demos with buzz).
3. **Cozy / satisfying / sim / clicker games: most small-channel hits have no name (N)** — Ant Mill, Company, Mycelarium (JustPatrick), Big Suck, Crack the Pack, Pack Ripper, Deforest, Frankenmine.
4. **Name inside the sentence (S) works for both**, especially "I Played 100% of X" — but that is FonFon / FungalBrainRot / JonnyBebop's own format (do not copy it).
5. **Hook first.** In every E hit the first words are a hook ("This is an Auto-Battler About…", "INCREDIBLE new incremental roguelike…", "The Only Thing Your Rats Remember…"). The name comes last.
6. **" | " and " - " both work** at the end (Ash/BryBry use " | ", Sharkinbear " - "). No sign that "|" hurts.
7. **Copying a winner's title fails** (SB Atomic 118 views with Ash Swoon's exact title).
8. **Our own channel** (tiny sample): top 3 are all survivor-like with the name in the sentence (I Am Plague ~1K, Outbreak Z ~680, Seabun ~430). Cozy games without the name: Way Too Many Fish ~36, Mycelarium low, Deforest 56.

**Limits:** vidIQ search shows the top results by views, not all videos. Views depend on age, channel and thumbnail too. Search traffic share per video is unknown (only the owner sees it). Treat this as a pattern, not proof. Re-check with our own search-traffic numbers at each /weekly-review.

| No name, cozy/sim | Way Too Many Fish, Mycelarium | 48 (22) | 115 | 56 |

- Cozy games got almost the same small search traffic **with or without** the name (17 vs 22 engaged). The description + tags already bring the name searches.
- The big search numbers came only from the **survivor-like games**, which also were early in their wave with few English videos. So genre + timing + name together made the difference, not the name alone.

**Decision (10 Nov 2026):** game name at the end (or inside the sentence) for survivor / horde / zombie / roguelite / action games that people search; story title without the name for cozy / satisfying / sim / clicker games; never the name first; never copy a title. Rule text: `playbook/titles.md` → "Game name: decide by game type".

### What the breakout channels have in common

1. **The breakout came from the right game at the right time, not from the title shape.** Yusterday's 245K and BryBry's 22.7K were each one video, posted on day 0–1 of a game that then exploded. Everything else on those channels gets 70–1K.
2. **Genre decides even inside one channel.** Ash Swoon uses the exact same title format every day: his zombie/horde videos get 8K–54K, his cozy/tidy-up videos 80–1.2K. **Zombie / horde defense incrementals are hot right now** (Ash has 9 zombie videos over 8K since 18 Oct).
3. **Each channel keeps one recognizable title format** (IdleAlex "I Have to…", JustPatrick "A Game Where…", Ash "This is a … | Game", FungalBrainRot "I Played 100% Of…").
4. **Most cozy/clicker breakouts use no game name (N)**, short first-person story with a concrete action + absurd object ("I Have to Clean Up a Bank Buried in Cash", "I Pushed Coins Until I Became a Millionaire"). The survivor/roguelite breakouts (Ash, BryBry) put the name at the end (E).
5. **Volume:** almost every growing small channel uploads **daily** (some twice a day, some 2–8 hour videos). Brimlo uploads 3–4 per week (his choice, full-time job). This is a fact to know, not a rule change.

**Result for Brimlo's title rule:** the per-genre rule above holds with 10 channels and 24 games. Biggest lever beyond the title: pick the hot game and publish on day 0–2 (already rules 11 + 12), and lean into zombie/horde incrementals while they are hot.
