This means our "big gold hook text + before→after" house style needs a test. It is **not proven wrong** (too few impressions on Rust & Shine), but the winners do the opposite. Next 3 videos: **one big object from the real game + one big number, no mascot, max 3 words.**

## 6. The new plan (do these in order)

### A. Every video: the first 30 seconds (most important)

1. **0:00** — Say the goal with a number, while already playing. Script:
   > "There are [1 quadrillion carrots / 1 million coins] in this game. I'm going to get all of them. Let's go."
2. **0:05** — Click / dig / shoot. No menus, no tour, no settings. Start the recording after the menus, or cut them.
3. **0:20** — Show where it ends (Seabun style): "In one hour I'll have [X]. This is how I got there."
4. Then the 10-second picture hook from `/hook` is **optional** — put it after the goal line, not before. (Guess: a still-picture intro before gameplay may make Browse viewers leave. Test it.)

### B. Every video: the edit

1. Cut all silence longer than 3 seconds. (DaVinci Resolve: "Detect silence" / Smart Reframe is not needed; CapCut has "Remove silences".)
2. Speed up grinding 4–8×, and say one live line before it: "Let me skip ahead a bit."
3. Don't aim for a length. The length is whatever is left after the cuts. Long is fine if it stays busy (your rule: long videos are OK).
4. Say the counter every few minutes: "We have 2 million now. Next goal: 1 billion."
5. Say one subscribe line near minute 2: "If you like watching numbers go up, subscribe. I finish a new one every few days."
6. One running joke per video. Plan it before recording. Simple English is fine (example: a name for your character, a "rival", or a sentence you repeat when something goes wrong).

### C. Titles: one steady formula (your own)

Main formula (fits incremental games, like LuckyLemon and Ridiculously Random):

> **I [Verb]-ed [BIG NUMBER in digits] [Thing] [short twist]**
> Example: "I Harvested 1,000,000,000,000,000 Carrots" · "I Scraped 1,000,000 Pieces of Dust With My Fingernail" · "I Restored 500 Rusty Antiques and Got Rich"

Rules:
- Big number in digits, not words.
- Game name goes in the description and tags, not in the title (unless the game is famous and searched, like a full release with big search volume).
- No "|" + game name at the end. No "Demo" (already a rule).
- Use this for the next 10 videos, then review.

### D. Game choice (keep what works)

- Keep picking **new** games (first 1–10 days). Plague, Carrots, Outbreak Z and Seabun were good picks.
- Avoid old, crowded games (Cookie Clicker, Digging a Hole) unless you have a new angle.
- Games both JonnyBebop and Azrilich already covered (Worming From Home, How to Fish, Dishwashing) are **old now** (Aug–Sep 2026). Do not chase them as vidIQ said.

### E. Upload speed

Keep **3 per week**. Use the 4th slot's time for the edit in part B. One tight 45-minute video beats two loose 90-minute videos. (vidIQ said 2; LuckyLemon/Azrilich do 7. For a solo creator with a job, 3 good ones is right.)

### F. How to know it works (check in Studio after 7 days)

| Metric | Now | Target for the next 3 videos |
|---|---|---|
| Retention at 1:00 (Engagement tab → hover the curve) | 17–45% | **60%+** |
| Avg view duration from Browse | 3:15 | **8:00+** |
| CTR from Browse | 2.5% | **4%+** |
| Avg % viewed | 4.8% | **15%+** |

If view duration goes up, YouTube keeps pushing, and views and subs follow. If CTR stays under 3% after the thumbnail change, we fix thumbnails next.
