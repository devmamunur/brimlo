## What all winners share

1. **One clear title style, used every time.** Nobody uses Brimlo's mix of a long story plus "| Game Name". Either the game name *is* the title ("I Played 100% of X") or there is no game name at all.
2. **The game is easy to say in 3 words:** coins, carrots, moon dust, paper, hay. Complex games (CG Grows) get a short theme line: "in this creepy mining incremental game".
3. **A floor.** Their worst videos still get 250–2,000 views. Brimlo's worst get 30–40. A floor like that comes from YouTube trusting the channel: viewers click and stay. That is CTR + retention, which only Studio shows.
4. **Length is not the problem.** FungalBrainRot posts 3–5 hour videos and gets 20K.
5. **Daily uploads are not required.** JonnyBebop posts ~1 per week and has the best median of the group.

## Suggestions

1. Titles without "| Game Name" (two shapes, see chat 6 Nov).
2. Prefer simple one-object games during their 7-day wave (rule already in channel/strategy.md).
3. Check retention at 1:00 for Pack Ripper, Ant Mill, Way Too Many Fish.
4. Keep 3–4 uploads per week. Do not chase daily.
