**What all four share:**
1. They **talk all the time**. No silent gaps.
2. They play **the game everyone is playing that week**, often within days of a big channel.
3. They have **one promise** you can say in one line, and every title and thumbnail repeats it.

**What none of them have:** special tags. LuckyLemon uses **zero tags** and still got 38K and 26K.

## 2. Each channel: what it does extra

### JonnyBebop — "100% playthroughs to sleep to"

- **Every** title: "I Played 100% of [Game]". **Every** thumbnail: the same drawn cartoon face of him + a "100%" badge in the corner, made by a paid artist ("Thumbnail Artist ➞ @El_Estofado" in every description).
- Channel description: "Full 100% playthroughs. **Gaming videos to sleep to.**"
- Comments prove the sleep audience: "putting it on before bed… calm but funny demeanor. subbed" / "add you to my bedtime video roster". **Long videos are a feature for this audience, not a problem.**
- **Full subtitles** on his videos. A viewer: "The whole video has subtitles?… you are the goat just for this alone."
- Talks 195–213 words per minute, jokes all the time. He cuts the end-grind openly: "let's just imagine I did it."
- Gets **developer keys**: "This game was given to us to try out in early access."
- Pins his own funny comment on every video ("I never thought a dishwashing game would be the one to break me…" — 97 likes).
- Also has Twitch, Instagram, X and a Discord. He may have had a small audience before YouTube (guess, not verified).
- Uploads ~2 per week. His hits were viral games: Dishwashing 112K, Bug Fighting 83K, Worming From Home 81K, How To Fish 80K.

### marqujj — first in Turkish, talks with every viewer

- Turkish creator living in Germany. Titles: "[Game]'ı %100 bitirdim!" ("I finished [Game] 100%!"). Channel line: "Chill oyunları %100 bitiriyoruz" ("We finish chill games 100%").
- **Language moat:** the same viral games as JonnyBebop, but for Turkish viewers. Comment: "I think you're the first in Turkey to fully finish it." There are almost no other Turkish channels for these games.
- **Replies to nearly every comment** (24 of 25 top comments had a reply). Pins a comment with an extra fact.
- **Asks for game ideas in every description**: "I consider every game suggestion. Let's meet in the comments!" Then plays them: "You suggested this game a lot in the comments."
- Result: **150–376 comments per video** (JonnyBebop: 44–92). Engagement 2–5% of views. **Best views → subs rate of the four (1.3%).**
- A **recurring character**: an "AI friend" called İsmişah, which viewers ask for again.
- Long, personal descriptions + **chapters** (gameplay starts at 0:15–0:23 in every chapter list).
- Comments say people watch "while eating" and "while falling asleep".

### Ridiculously Random — volume + one hit

- 26 long videos in 30 days. Middle length ~40 min. Titles "[I + verb] [simple thing] Until [HUGE]!!!". Thumbnails: big yellow word/number + red arrow.
- The jump came from **one video** (Inbox Zero, 47K, 8 Oct). After 1K subs (25 Oct) growth slowed: 1,020 → 1,040 in 3 days. New videos: 59–9K views.
- Writes **"CURRENT SUBS: [N]"** and a personal line ("ALMOST AT 1000 LETS GO") in every description.
- **Reads the game's dialogue in a funny voice** and jokes with the characters ("Fair enough, Gary, mate"). So he reads text, but makes it a show. He never goes quiet.
- 25–32 tags, including junk ones ("asmr", "speedrun"). Long AI-style keyword paragraph.

### LuckyLemon — right game, right day, first video

- Channel created **16 Oct 2026**. First video the same day: **One Million Coins, 38K**. Azrilich posted the same game **8 hours earlier** (186K). Second video: **Quadrillion Carrots on 18 Oct, 26K** (same day as ours: 71).
- Titles: "I [did] 1,000,000 [thing] And I Regret Nothing". Thumbnails: one huge clean 3D picture (a pile of hay, a field of bricks) + one big number. A comment: "I assumed you had at least a million subs."
- Opening: the goal in sentence 1, action at 0:05, says the counter out loud ("300 coins out of 1 million").
- Uses Epidemic Sound music under the commentary.
- **Zero tags.** Two-line description.
- **Weak spots:** after the two hot games, videos got 191–2,066 views. Only 293 subs from 65K views (0.45%, the worst rate here). No replies to comments. **So LuckyLemon proves that the hot game + opening gets views, but without a community the views do not turn into subs.**

## 3. Pattern 1 — They ride the "game wave"

The same small set of games appears on all these channels within days:

| Game | Who played it (date → views) |
|---|---|
| One Million Coins | Azrilich 16 Oct → 186K · **LuckyLemon 16 Oct (8 h later) → 38K** · JonnyBebop 25 Oct → 14K |
| Quadrillion Carrots | **LuckyLemon 18 Oct → 26K** · Brimlo 18 Oct → 71 · Azrilich 22 Oct → 77K · Ridiculously Random 22 Oct → 9.4K |
| Find the Needle | marqujj 2 Oct → 69K · JonnyBebop 7 Oct → 57K (also Jake Unlocked 503K, Factoristic 107K in Sep) |
| Crack the Pack (card packs) | Azrilich 8 Oct → 188K · JonnyBebop 16 Oct → 41K |
| Scrapecremental | Ridiculously Random 25 Oct → 4.7K in 2 days · Brimlo 27 Oct → 6 in 1 day |
| Worming From Home | JonnyBebop 5 Sep → 81K · marqujj 16 Sep → 20K |
| Digging a Hole | marqujj 11 Sep → 33K · Brimlo 13 Oct → 28 (a month late) |
| Bowling / Piñata | Azrilich 15–16 Sep → 140K / 33K · Ridiculously Random 19 / 24 Oct → 2.8K / 1K (a month late) |

**What this shows:**
- When a game is hot, **the first 1–7 days** pay the most. A month later, even Ridiculously Random only gets 1–3K.
- Small channels next to a big channel's video on the same game probably get **suggested-video traffic** from it (my guess: I can't see their Studio, but the timing fits).
- **Search demand is real for hot game names** (vidIQ, 28 Oct): "find the needle game" ~106K searches/month (+358%), "quadrillion carrots" ~31K, "one million coins game" ~8.9K, **"scrapecremental" ~11K with very low competition (12/100)**.

**For Brimlo:** you already pick hot games (Carrots, Scrapecremental). The gap was not the game. It was the first 2 minutes and the silence (see the head-to-head review). But you should also **watch Azrilich and JonnyBebop every day**. When they post a new incremental game you can run, record it within 1–3 days (🔥 FAST label).

Never junk tags.

## 10. What not to copy

- **Daily uploads** (RR, LuckyLemon). Not possible next to your job. 3 good videos a week is right.
- **A paid thumbnail artist** (JonnyBebop). Not now. Maybe after YPP.
- **Junk tags and AI keyword paragraphs** (RR).
- **"I Played 100% of"** word for word (JonnyBebop's brand).
- **Switching language** (marqujj). A Bengali channel would have little competition, but much lower ad pay and it is not our plan (US audience). The real lesson from marqujj is: **be first in your lane, and talk with every viewer.**
