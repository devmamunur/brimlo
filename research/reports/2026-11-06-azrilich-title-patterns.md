# Azrilich title patterns (+ LuckyLemon)

Date: Fri 6 Nov 2026. Data: `brimlo_channel_videos` @AzrilichIdle, last 50 uploads (8 Sep – 5 Nov 2026), cached 5 Nov. Channel: 78.7K subs, median 91,903 views per video.

## Facts

- 50 of 50 titles: **no game name**, no "incremental", no "|", no "demo".
- Median length **39 characters / 7 words** (max 61).
- 30 of 50 start with "I" (first person, past tense).
- Only 7 of 50 have digits. Numbers are used when the number *is* the joke.
- All lowercase (his signature). Almost no CAPS.
- The title tells a tiny story, a joke or a strange command. It never describes the game.

## Patterns and best examples

| Pattern | Examples (views) |
|---|---|
| I [did a crazy thing] [funny result] | I made the grim reaper look unemployed (604K) · I printed so much money I scared the king (408K) · I sacrificed an entire frog civilization to a hole (187K) · I became addicted to opening card packs (208K) |
| I [did] [BIG NUMBER] [thing] | I got paid to destroy 100 quintillion dollars (274K) · I can't leave until I pick up 1,000,000 coins (200K) · one quadrillion carrots (93K) |
| This [thing] isn't going to [verb] itself | this lake isn't going to empty itself (316K) |
| They made me [huge job] | they made me sort the entire ocean (123K) · they made me pick up all this money (64K) |
| I had to [goal] before [threat] | I had to spend $1 billion before the tax man took it (248K) |
| A game where being [X] gets you [Y] | a game where being adorable gets you canned (192K) · …useless gets you promoted (76K) |
| You must / the [thing] must be fed | you must roll 3 in a row (137K) · the fire must be fed (100K) · you must feed the flesh wall (36K) |
| Superlative (rare) | this is the most satisfying game of the year (225K) · this is the best tower defense game of the year (32K) |

Weaker ones (13–33K) are often vague: "my ship got too overpowered", "I turned my castle into a tank", "don't let their cuteness fool you". No clear goal or absurd scale.

## LuckyLemon (same shape, Title Case)

"I Farmed 1,000,000,000,000,000 Carrots And I Regret Nothing" (45K), "A Game About Collecting 1,000,000 Coins And It's Way Too Satisfying" (46K), "I Cleaned 1,000,000,000 Tons Of Moon Dust" (10.7K). Misses have no number and no clear goal.

## Decision

Mamunur, 6 Nov 2026: Brimlo uses this system from now on. Rules and the 7 patterns: [playbook/titles.md](../../playbook/titles.md). Our own look: Title Case (not lowercase), own words, true to the video.

## Update 6 Nov 2026: title tests + thumbnails

**Titles change after upload.** Mamunur's screenshots (6 Nov) show other titles than the API: "just one more bottle flip…" → "i flipped bottles until i lost my mind"; "how many tickets does it take to win the lottery?" → "I scratched lottery tickets until I won"; "the ocean is full of trash and I have to catch it all" → "I became a robot to clean up humanity's trash problem"; "I dug too deep on mars" → "they shouldn't have let me dig on mars"; "the fire must be fed" → "I had to burn 1,000,000,000 papers". Probably YouTube Test & Compare (different viewers see different titles), or renames. The new ones are more "I [did X] until [result]" and more concrete.

**Thumbnails (12 checked):** 11 of 12 have no text; only in-game words (ticket "FIND", paper "NOTICE OF TERMINATION").
