## Rules seen in all 187 titles

- **Number:** 147 of 187 (79%) have a number. 81 (43%) have a `$` amount. Digits, never words.
- **Length:** median **33 characters, 6 words** (shortest 17, longest 55).
- **Comma split** (`Do X, Win Y`): 33 titles. The main channel uses it more and more in 2025–2026.
- **First person** (I / We / My): 35 titles (19%). Most titles are about the challenge, not about "me".
- **Game name:** only 28 titles (15%), all on Gaming (Minecraft, GTA, Among Us). The idea sells, not the game.
- **"!" at the end:** 74 titles, mostly before 2025. Newer titles mostly drop it.
- **Questions:** only 6. He rarely asks; he states.
- **Weak ones:** "1 Hour Of …" compilations (0.22–0.27), charity / no-stakes titles (I Built 10 Schools 0.49, Hi Me In 10 Years 0.38), and repeats of a pattern that already ran (10 YouTubers vs **2** Secret Traitors 0.58 after the "1 Secret Traitor" video got 1.36).

## What Brimlo can use (and what not)

- **No real-money stakes.** "Win $100,000" only works because his money is real. Use **true in-game numbers** instead.
- **Already in our system:** SURVIVED ≈ P5 · GRIND ("Mining 1,000,000 Blocks Alone") = P1 exactly.
- **Worth testing (as B swap titles first):**
  - **VS (small vs huge):** "1 Coin vs 1 Trillion Coins" · "Day 1 vs Hour 2 Factory" — shows "numbers go up" in one line.
  - **EVERY:** "Every Upgrade Doubles My Money" · "Every Minute The Numbers Get Bigger" — best median breakout (1.18).
  - **WIN comma shape** with a game goal: "Reach 1 Trillion Coins, Beat The Game" — challenge + finish in one line.
- **Avoid:** "1 Hour Of [game]" style titles (his worst pattern), and running the same title shape twice in a row.
