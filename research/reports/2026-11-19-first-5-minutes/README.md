## Part 2 — What the numbers say

### 1. The silly story start is how our niche starts now

In our niche (Azrilich, LuckyLemon, Ridiculously Random, Malakye G, mcveety, FonFon = 30 videos):

| Start type | Videos | Median views |
|---|---|---|
| **S — silly story reason** | 16 | **~147K** |
| G — goal with a number | 6 | ~63K |
| N — game name / store page | 8 | ~26K |

**Careful:** this does not prove the start alone causes the views. Bigger channels (mcveety, FonFon, Azrilich) use S, so channel size matters too. But **all three of the fastest-growing channels use S in 15 of 15 videos**: FonFon (27.6K subs, 2.7M-view hit), mcveety (37K subs, two videos over 500K) and Azrilich. LuckyLemon and Ridiculously Random use G. Malakye G uses N and is the weakest per video, except when he is first in a wave.

**The S formula, word for word from the top videos:**
- FonFon (2.7M): "I was locked in a room with a giant stack of hay. **So, to escape** this place and 100% this game, **I'm going to have to** sift through endless piles of hay by hand, build a fully automated factory… just to find a single needle. **So enjoy the video and let's get sifting.**"
- mcveety (540K, Drink the Ocean): "Someone's drowning at the bottom of this ocean. **So to save them, I just need to**…" (already drinking)
- FonFon (630K): "I was hiking one day when I stumbled upon an abandoned village. **So I decided** I will rebuild 100% of the buildings brick by brick…"

The shape is always **[something happened to me] → So [the goal] → (FonFon adds: the journey promise "build a factory just to…") → let's go.**

### 2. Nobody asks for subscribes at the start

In 50 videos, only **2** ask for a like or subscribe in the first 5 minutes: Techno Gamerz at 0:32 ("this video needs 600,000 likes") and Azrilich at 4:31. Our playbook's "Subscribe line ~2:00" is not what the niche does. **Move it later** (after the first big upgrade, around 5–10 minutes), or drop it.

### 3. They talk nonstop, but speed is not the main problem

- Niche channels: about **180–250 words/min**. FonFon talks the fastest (240–258).
- Calm channels that still do well: Wanderbots 123–161 and Malakye G 143–173.
- **Brimlo: 155 (Drink the Ocean) and 127 (Big Ice).**

So we're within the calm range. **Don't try to talk faster**; with English as a second language it would sound forced. The real problem is **silence and guessing** ("maybe… maybe… what is this"). The fix is short sentences said more often: "Okay." "Nice!" "Oh no." "$48. Good."

### 4. Minutes 2–5 are a loop

The top videos (Azrilich 663K, mcveety 540K, FonFon 2.7M) all repeat the same loop between 2:00 and 5:00:

1. **Collect / do the action** while talking.
2. **Say the number:** "I'm up to 44", "That was $12", "$1.70 each".
3. **Choose the next upgrade and say why:** "I want that to happen way more often", "a better straw to make this easier".
4. **Buy it and test it right away.**
5. **Compare before and after:** "Before, a full shovel gave $2.22. Now…" / "Oh, that's so quick."
6. **Remind the big goal:** "13 million strands of hay. We better get to work." / "We're going to be here all day."
7. A **small joke or reaction** every 20–30 seconds.

The first upgrade is bought **before 1:30** in most niche videos.

### 5. Techno Gamerz (11–17M views): a story, not a game tour

Techno Gamerz is a different kind of channel (huge Indian audience, GTA/sims/horror), but three lessons transfer:
- **He tells the backstory like a film narrator:** "3 years ago I had a gas station in the middle of a desert…", "Last night a cyclone destroyed this island…"
- **He makes problems into small dramas:** "I have 3 cars, only one can escape", "My only worker Samuel didn't leave… because I still owe him $99."
- **He talks to the viewer all the time:** "brother, look", "you can see". Even the game's tutorial tasks become part of the story ("Mom said check the glass first").

### 6. Being early can beat a bad start

Malakye G read the Drink the Ocean store page for a full minute (the worst start in the set) and still got **86K**. He posted on **Fri 14 Nov 2026, 00:46 UTC**, one of the first in the wave. Brimlo posted the same game about one day later with a better start and got 1.6K. **The start decides how many people stay; the timing decides how many people come.** Both matter. Rule 11 (48-hour launch window) stays the top priority.

## Part 4 — THE BRIMLO GUIDE: first 5 minutes

The rules from this guide now live in [playbook/hooks-and-scripts.md](../../../playbook/hooks-and-scripts.md) and the [script card template](../../../playbook/script-card/template.md). It fits a calm voice and simple English.

### Before recording (3 minutes, on the script card)

1. Test the game for 5–10 minutes (you already do this). Find **the goal**, **the first action** and **the first upgrade**.
2. Write **3 short lines**:
   - **Line 1 — The reason:** something silly happened to you. *"I was locked in a room with…" / "My grandpa left me…" / "My boss gave me one straw and said…"*
   - **Line 2 — The goal (with "So"):** *"So, to get out, I have to [goal with a number]."*
   - **Line 3 — The journey + go:** *"I'll start with [tiny tool]. One day I'll have [big machine]. Let's get [verb]-ing."*
3. **Start a new save.** Begin recording on the first real game screen.

### 0:00–0:15 — Reason → Goal → Go

Say the 3 lines. **Start the first action while you say Line 2.**

> Pawn It example: *"My grandpa left me his old pawn shop. It's dirty and nobody comes. So I'm going to make it the best shop in town. Right now I have $0 and one dusty shelf. Let's get selling."*

### 0:15–1:30 — First loop

Action → say the number → first upgrade (read its name once, say what it does in simple words, buy it) → test it → "Oh, that's faster!"

### 1:30–5:00 — Keep looping

Repeat the loop from Part 2, point 4: **do → number → choose upgrade + why → buy → test → before/after → remind the goal.**

Use these short lines, which you can say without thinking:
- Number: *"$48. Nice." / "That's 0.1% done." / "I'm up to 200."*
- Why: *"I want this one. It makes me faster." / "I'll save for the big one."*
- Before/after: *"Before: 5 at a time. Now: 8. Much better."*
- Goal reminder: *"Still a long way to go." / "One day this will be five stars."*
- Reaction: *"Oh no." / "Wait, what?" / "Yes!" / "Okay, okay."*

### Don'ts (from the data)

1. Don't say "This is the game named…" or read the store page. The title already says it.
2. Don't do a map or menu tour.
3. Don't say "maybe, maybe" with no action. Try it instead: *"I don't know what this does. Let's try."*
4. **No subscribe ask in the first 5 minutes.** If you want one, put it after the first big upgrade (~5–10 min), tied to the story: *"Want to see the shop at five stars? Subscribe and stay."*
5. Don't try to talk faster. Talk more often with short lines.

### How we check it

On the next 3 videos, compare **retention at 2:00 and at 5:00** with Drink the Ocean (14% at 2:00). The 5:00 number is new, so take it from Studio.
