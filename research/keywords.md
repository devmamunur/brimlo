## Lessons

- "incremental" > "idle" in titles.
- Most views will come from **suggested / browse** next to bigger creators — covering new games quickly is how you show up there.
- Many "X incremental" terms are **Roblox** games or dev tutorials. Avoid them.
- Never use creator names as tags.
