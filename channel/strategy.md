# Strategy — how Brimlo wins

Last updated: 13 Oct 2026. Based on [the deep research report](../research/reports/Idle%20gaming%20channel%20playbook.md) (vidIQ data 12–13 Oct 2026).

## The big idea

Long, calm, live-commentary videos of **new incremental games**, posted **early**, packaged with a clear **"tiny → huge"** promise.

Growth in this niche comes from **hits**: 1–3 videos that do 10–40x better than normal. You can't predict which one. So:
1. Make **more good chances** (more videos, each with a good game + title + thumbnail).
2. Be **early** on new games (demos, playtests, launches — first 1–10 days).
3. Make each video **easy to click**: one strange idea, one clear promise.

## Niche (decided 13 Oct 2026)

One channel, two game types. Both must have **upgrades and growing numbers**:
1. **Classic incremental / idle / survivor-like** (rat swarm, mining, tower defense).
2. **Satisfying job-sim with upgrades** (cleaning, restoring, junkyard, metal detector, organizing) — what Azrilich plays; Mamunur loves these and viewers want both.

Aim for roughly 60% type 1 and 40% type 2 at first; shift to whichever gets more views after 10–20 videos. No second channel (see strategy: one clear idea, double work, double watch-hour bar). Plain simulators with no progression (e.g. Supermarket Simulator) stay at one video.

## Format rules

- **One game per video.** 60–120 minutes. Never stretch to fill time.
- **Live commentary.** Talk about what's on screen and why you choose things. Silence is fine for a few seconds, not minutes.
- **Light edit:** cut loading, menus, mistakes, dead time. Speed up idle waits (x4–x16) with a small label.
- **No multi-part series.** (Idle Niv stalled after a 15-part series.)
- Second video from one game only if it's different: demo now → "100% full release" later, or a big update.

## Frequency

| Period | Videos / week | How |
|---|---|---|
| Now → 8 Nov 2026 | 2 (Tue + Fri) | One video per weekend day |
| 9 Nov → early Jan | 3 (Tue + Fri + Sun) | Saturday session makes **two** 60–90 min videos on two different games |
| After 12-week review | Test 4 only if energy is good | Decide on 3 Jan 2027 |
| Any time | +1 "fast lane" | Hot demo/playtest/launch → publish in 1–3 days |

**Why not 2 forever?** Every growing small channel in this niche posts 4–7 long videos a week. None was found growing on 1–3. **Why not 5 now?** Full-time job + burnout risk (52–62% of creators report burnout). A channel that stops gets nothing.

- **Nerfed Reflexes** — "chill" brand, replies to every comment, developers pitch games to them.
- **MOREQUIS** — "I 100% …" and giant-number titles; 1.5–2h videos tripled the channel.

## Packaging

- Put **"incremental"** in most titles (search "incremental game" +71%; "idle games" −54%).
- Pure incremental → sell the idea, game name in description.
- Hyped launch / Next Fest demo / roguelite / tower defense → add "| Game Name" or "| First Look Demo" at the end.
- Thumbnails: one idea, 2–4 words or one big number, before→after or 0%→100%.

## Games

- Themes that break out: **dark/horror**, **swarm/army from nothing**, **cozy satisfying cleanup**, **mining/digging/fishing**.
- Free first: Steam demos, playtests, free-to-play incrementals, developer keys (Keymailer, email).
- Buy max **$10–15/month**, on sale, only after the checklist passes.

## Money path (honest)

- YPP for new applicants from 1 Feb 2027: **1,000 subs + 8,000 watch hours** (365 days). Brimlo will not reach the old 4,000-hour bar before then.
- 8,000 hours ≈ 35,600 views at 90 min and ~15% watched. That is ~340 views per video at 2/week — **or one big hit**.
- Expect 12–18 months without a hit, 3–6 months with one. Plan energy for the long case.
- RPM for US gaming long-form ≈ $2–6 per 1,000 views (median ~$3.50).

## Sim games "in my own way"

Azrilich is the **game-choice and title-shape model**, not the copy target. Brimlo does it differently:

| Azrilich | Brimlo (my way) |
|---|---|
| Fast edit, jokes, ~20–50 min, ~1 video/day | Calm live commentary, 60–120 min, 2–3 videos/week |
| Skits and fast-talking | Explain what's on screen, why I choose, honest verdict |
| Shows the "wow" result | Shows the **whole journey** and tells viewers if the game is worth buying |

Brimlo's promise: *calm, honest, full-length — no fast cuts, see everything.* This is also the lower-risk path for monetization (real commentary, real gameplay).
- Use his **title shape** (template I) and **game ideas**, in my own words.
- Do not copy his exact titles or thumbnails.
- Keep the first-30-seconds hook and the "tiny → huge" promise.
